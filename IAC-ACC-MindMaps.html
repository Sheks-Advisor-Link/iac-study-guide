IFRS Mind-Maps — Journals & Audit Assertions

IAC 2026 · T. VAN HEERDEN

Scope / Excluded

  • Excludes: financial instruments (IFRS 9); biological assets (IAS 41)
  • Producers of agri/forest products & commodity broker-traders → measured at NRV / FV less costs to sell

Definition

  • Assets held for sale in the ordinary course
  • In the process of production for such sale
  • Materials/supplies to be consumed in production

Cost includes

  • Purchase cost (less trade discount)
  • Conversion cost (labour + production overheads)
  • Costs to bring to location & condition
  • Exclude: abnormal waste, storage, admin, selling
IAS 2Inventories

Cost formula

  • FIFO or weighted average (no LIFO)
  • Standard cost / retail method ok if ≈ cost
  • Same formula for similar inventories

NRV

  • Lower of cost and NRV
  • NRV = selling price − costs to complete − costs to sell
  • Written down when damaged/obsolete; tested each year-end

Key tips

  • Inventory sold → cost of sales (P/L)
  • Write-down → P/L; reversal limited to original write-down

Audit assertions

  • Completeness low — all inventory recorded; risk of understating assets → attend stock count (floor→sheet).
  • Existence high — recorded inventory exists; overstatement risk → stock count (sheet→floor).
  • Valuation high — at lower of cost/NRV; estimation risk → invoice inspection & NRV recalculation.
  • Rights low — owned, not consignment → inspect contracts/GRNs.
  • Cut-off high — right period → last GRNs/dispatches around year-end.
  • Presentation low — disclosed per IAS 2 → AFS vs GL comparison.

IAS 16 — Property, Plant & Equipment

IAC 2026

Definition / Classify

  • Tangible items held for use in production/supply/rental/admin
  • Expected to be used >1 period
  • Component approach (significant parts)

Initial recognition

  • Probable future benefits AND
  • Cost measured reliably

Initial measurement = cost

  • Purchase price − trade discount
  • + directly attributable (delivery, install, test, site prep)
  • + PV dismantling/restoration
  • + borrowing costs (IAS 23)
IAS 16Property, Plant & Equipment

Subsequent — models

  • Cost model: cost − accum dep − impairment
  • Revaluation: FV − later dep − impairment (whole class)

Depreciation

  • Over useful life to residual value
  • Review UL/residual/method annually (estimate → prospective)

Key tips

  • Reval ↑ → OCI (surplus); ↓ → P/L (unless reverses surplus)
  • Deferred tax on revaluation → OCI

Audit assertions

  • Existence high — assets exist → physically inspect register→floor.
  • Completeness low — all recorded → floor→register; scan repairs for capital items.
  • Rights low — owned → title deeds, vehicle papers, invoices.
  • Valuation high — cost, depreciation & impairment correct → recalc depreciation; reval = valuer's report; impairment indicators.
  • Cut-off low — additions/disposals in right period.
  • Presentation low — IAS 16 disclosure (reconciliation, policy) → AFS review.

IAS 40 — Investment Property

IAC 2026

Definition / Classify

  • Land/building held to earn rentals or for capital appreciation
  • NOT owner-occupied (IAS 16), NOT for sale (IAS 2/IFRS 5)

Recognition

  • Probable benefits + cost reliable (as IAS 16)

Initial measurement

  • Cost + transaction costs
IAS 40Investment Property

Subsequent — models

  • Fair value model: remeasure to FV; changes → P/L; no depreciation
  • Cost model: as IAS 16
  • Policy applies to ALL IP

Change of use

  • Transfer in/out when use changes (e.g. owner-occupied → IP)
  • FV model: remeasure at transfer date

Key tips

  • FV model = no depreciation (common error)
  • Deferred tax on FV gains

  • Existence/Rights low — exists & owned → title deeds; physical inspection.
  • Valuation high — fair value reasonable → independent valuer (competence/objectivity); recompute; market comparison.
  • Completeness low — all IP recorded → fixed-asset register; minutes.
  • Classification high — IP vs owner-occupied vs inventory → inspect use; lease agreements.
  • Presentation low — IAS 40 disclosure (model, FV method, assumptions).

IAS 38 — Intangible Assets

IAC 2026

Definition / Classify

  • Identifiable (separable OR contractual/legal) non-monetary asset with no physical substance
  • Controlled; future benefits

Recognition

  • Probable benefits + cost reliable
  • Research → expense
  • Development → capitalise if PIRATE
  • Internally-gen goodwill/brands → no

Initial measurement

  • Cost (purchased) or directly attributable development cost
IAS 38Intangible Assets

Subsequent — models

  • Cost or revaluation (only if active market)
  • Finite: amortise over UL
  • Indefinite: no amortisation, annual impairment

PIRATE (capitalise dev.)

  • Probable benefits · Intention to complete · Resources · Ability to use/sell · Technical feasibility · Expenditure measurable

Key tips

  • Past research expensed → cannot reinstate
  • Amortisation starts when available for use

  • Existence/Rights low/high — intangible exists & owned → registration/contracts (high risk = no physical form).
  • Valuation high — cost & amortisation/impairment → recalc; impairment for indefinite-life.
  • Recognition (Dev.) high — PIRATE met before capitalising → inspect evidence of each criterion.
  • Completeness low — all recorded.
  • Presentation low — IAS 38 disclosure (finite/indefinite, UL).

IAS 36 — Impairment of Assets

IAC 2026

Scope

  • Most non-financial assets: PPE, intangibles, goodwill, investments in subs/associates

Trigger (when to test)

  • Indicators: external (market, rates) / internal (damage, obsolescence, worse performance)
  • Annual: goodwill & indefinite-life intangibles

Measure

  • Impairment = carrying amount − recoverable amount
  • Recoverable = higher of FV less costs of disposal & value in use (PV future CF, pre-tax rate)
IAS 36Impairment

CGU

  • Test the cash-generating unit if no independent cash flows
  • Allocate loss: goodwill first, then pro-rata

Reversal

  • Reverse if indicators gone — limited to depreciated historical cost
  • Goodwill: never reversed

Key tips

  • VIU uses pre-tax rate & pre-financing cash flows
  • Exclude future restructuring not yet committed

Key journals

Recognise impairment
Dr Impairment loss (P/L)x
  Cr Accum impairmentx
Reversal (not goodwill)
Dr Asset / Accum impairmentx
  Cr Reversal of impairment (P/L)x

Audit assertions

  • Valuation high — recoverable amount correct → re-perform VIU; assess discount rate vs peers; agree cash flows to budgets/contracts.
  • Completeness high — all impairment indicators identified → discuss with mgmt; review industry/market.
  • Accuracy high — calc & allocation (goodwill first) → recalc; trace JE to GL.
  • Presentation low — IAS 36 disclosure (assumptions, sensitivities).
  • Mgmt bias high — assess optimism in assumptions; back-test prior estimates.

IFRS 5 — Non-current Assets Held for Sale

IAC 2026

Classify as HFS

  • Carrying amount recovered principally through sale, not use
  • Available for immediate sale in present condition

"Highly probable"

  • Committed plan + active marketing
  • Reasonable price
  • Sale expected within 12 months

Measure

  • Lower of carrying amount & fair value less costs to sell
  • Stop depreciating
IFRS 5Held for Sale

Presentation

  • Separately as current asset (and liabilities of disposal group)

Discontinued op

  • Component = separate major line/geographic area
  • Results shown separately (one line, P/L)

Key tips

  • Fails criteria → reclassify back at lower of CA (had it not been HFS) & recoverable amount

Audit assertions

  • Classification high — HFS criteria met → inspect board approval, marketing, plan.
  • Valuation high — lower of CA & FVLCS → valuer report; recompute; check depreciation stopped.
  • Existence/Rights low — title deeds; inspection.
  • Presentation high — separate presentation & discontinued-op disclosure → AFS review.

IFRS 16 — Leases (lessee)

IAC 2026

Definition / Classify

  • Contract conveys right to control use of an identified asset for a period for consideration
  • Exemptions: short-term ≤12m, low-value → expense

Recognition

  • At commencement: recognise ROU asset + lease liability

Initial measurement

  • Liability = PV of lease payments @ implicit rate (or IBR)
  • ROU = liability + IDC + prepayments + dismantling − incentives
IFRS 16Leases

Subsequent

  • Liability: amortised cost (+interest −payments)
  • ROU: cost − depreciation − impairment (shorter of term/UL)

Lessor

  • Finance lease: derecognise asset → net investment (receivable)
  • Operating: keep asset, rental income

Key tips

  • Payments incl. RV guarantees, options reasonably certain
  • Split each payment: interest vs capital

  • Completeness high — all leases recognised → scan rentals in GL for off-balance leases.
  • Valuation high — PV of liability & ROU correct → recompute discounting; agree inputs to contract.
  • Rights/Obligations low — signed lease; lessee is entity.
  • Classification high — exemptions applied correctly → assess low-value/short-term.
  • Presentation low — IFRS 16 maturity & expense disclosure.

IFRS 15 — Revenue from Contracts with Customers

IAC 2026

Step 1–2

  • Identify the contract (approved, rights, payment terms, commercial substance)
  • Identify performance obligations (distinct goods/services)

Step 3–4

  • Transaction price (variable consideration + constraint, financing, non-cash)
  • Allocate by relative stand-alone selling price

Step 5 — recognise

  • When/as PO satisfied = control transfers
  • Over time (3 criteria) or point in time (5 indicators)
IFRS 15Revenue

Over time criteria

  • Customer consumes as performed; or
  • Creates/enhances asset customer controls; or
  • No alternative use + right to payment

Special

  • Principal (gross) vs agent (net)
  • Contract asset / liability (deferred income)
  • Right of return → refund liability

Key tips

  • Control, not risks & rewards, drives timing
  • Warranties: assurance vs service

Audit assertions

  • Occurrence high — revenue is real (fraud risk) → agree to delivery notes/contracts.
  • Completeness low — all sales recorded → trace dispatches to invoices.
  • Accuracy high — correct amount & allocation → recompute; price list.
  • Cut-off high — right period (when control transfers) → inspect delivery dates around y/e.
  • Presentation low — IFRS 15 disaggregation disclosure.

IFRS 9 — Financial Instruments

IAC 2026

Classify (assets)

  • Business model + SPPI test
  • Amortised cost / FVOCI (debt) / FVTPL
  • Equity: FVTPL default; FVOCI election (no recycling)

Initial measurement

  • Fair value + transaction costs
  • (FVTPL: costs → P/L)

Subsequent

  • Amortised cost = effective interest
  • FVOCI debt: FV→OCI; interest/ECL/FX→P/L; recycle
  • FVTPL: FV→P/L
IFRS 9Financial Instruments

Impairment (ECL)

  • 3-stage expected credit loss model
  • Simplified (lifetime ECL) for trade receivables

Liabilities

  • Amortised cost (default) or FVTPL
  • Own-credit FV change → OCI

Key tips

  • Derecognise when rights expire / transferred with risks
  • Hedge accounting optional

IAS 37 — Provisions, Contingent Liabilities & Assets

IAC 2026

Classify

  • Provision: liability of uncertain timing/amount
  • Contingent liability: possible / not probable → disclose
  • Contingent asset: disclose if probable

Recognise provision (ALL)

  • Present obligation (legal/constructive) from past event
  • Probable outflow
  • Reliable estimate

Measure

  • Best estimate of expenditure
  • Expected value (large pop) / most likely (single)
  • Discount if material
IAS 37Provisions

Special cases

  • Onerous contract → provide unavoidable net cost
  • Restructuring → detailed plan + announced
  • No future operating losses

Reimbursement

  • Recognise asset only if virtually certain; ≤ provision

Key tips

  • Unwind discount → finance cost
  • Review & adjust each year-end

Key journals

  Cr Provisionx

Audit assertions

  • Completeness high — all obligations provided → minutes, legal letters, post-y/e payments.
  • Valuation high — best estimate → recompute; expert/legal confirmation; discount rate.
  • Existence/Obligation high — present obligation exists → inspect contracts, correspondence.
  • Presentation high — provision vs contingent disclosure → AFS review.

IAS 12 — Income Taxes (incl. Deferred Tax)

IAC 2026

Components

  • Current tax (per Tax Act on taxable profit)
  • Deferred tax (temporary differences)

Deferred tax =

  • (Carrying amount − tax base) × tax rate
  • Taxable temp diff → DTL
  • Deductible temp diff / assessed loss → DTA (if probable profit)

Tax base

  • Asset: future deductible amount
  • Liability: CA − future deductible
IAS 12Income Taxes

Rate

  • Expected rate when it reverses
  • Capital items → CGT inclusion (e.g. 80%) → effective rate

Exemptions

  • Initial recognition exemption
  • Tax on OCI/equity items → OCI/equity

Key tips

  • DTA only if probable future taxable profit (judgement)
  • Offset only if legal right

IAS 21 — Foreign Exchange

IAC 2026

Key terms

  • Functional currency = currency of the primary economic environment
  • Presentation currency may differ

Initial

  • Record foreign transaction at the spot rate on transaction date

Year-end — monetary

  • Monetary items (cash, debtors, creditors, loans) → closing rate
  • Differences → P/L
IAS 21Foreign Exchange

Year-end — non-monetary

  • At cost → historical rate
  • At fair value → rate when FV measured

Foreign operation

  • Assets/liabilities → closing rate
  • Income → average rate
  • Differences → OCI (FCTR), recycled on disposal

Key tips

  • Forex on a capital asset (pre-use) may defer (link IAS 16/IAS 23)

  • Classification high — monetary vs non-monetary; P/L vs OCI (FCTR).
  • Presentation low — IAS 21 disclosure (functional currency, FCTR).

IAS 8 Policies/Estimates/Errors  ·  IAS 10 Events After Reporting

IAC 2026

IAS 8 — policy change

  • Apply retrospectively (restate comparatives & opening RE)
  • Only if required by a standard or more relevant/reliable

IAS 8 — estimate change

  • Prospective (current & future)
  • e.g. useful life, residual, ECL, provision

IAS 8 — prior error

  • Retrospective restatement (restate comparatives & opening balances)
IAS 8 / 10Policies · Events

IAS 10 — adjusting

  • Evidence of conditions at year-end → adjust AFS
  • e.g. debtor insolvency, NRV, court ruling, error

IAS 10 — non-adjusting

  • Arose after year-end → disclose only
  • e.g. fire, dividend declared, share issue

Key tips

  • Going concern lost after y/e → always adjust the basis
  • Dividends declared after y/e → not a liability

Audit assertions / focus

  • Accuracy high — restatement computed & classified correctly → recompute; trace to comparatives.
  • Completeness high — all subsequent events identified → review minutes, post-y/e records to report date.
  • Classification high — policy vs estimate vs error; adjusting vs non-adjusting → assess nature.
  • Presentation high — IAS 8/10 disclosure (nature, amount, reason).
