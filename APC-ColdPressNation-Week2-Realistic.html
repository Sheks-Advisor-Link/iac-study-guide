Reflection points (Step 3)

Comparing to my Week-1 preparation

  • Preparation paid off where I researched the trigger deeply. The two C's — the agriculture value note (Task 3) and the audit of agricultural produce (Task 8) — are exactly the triggers I unpacked in Week 1. Deep prep on a trigger = competence.
  • I under-researched depth on the hard sub-issues. I identified the loan and tax triggers, but not deeply enough to handle s8F hybrid debt / s19 (Task 2) or the IAS 32 compound-instrument split (Task 1). Identifying a trigger isn't enough — I must research it to the level of its hardest sub-question.
  • I missed the sharpness of two triggers. The ethics/greenwashing angle (Task 4) and the employees'-tax control test (Task 6) were live in the pre-release but I didn't anticipate them precisely.

Where the marks actually leaked (and the fix)

  • Calculations & showing workings (Task 5). Include all variable costs (don't forget selling & distribution), drop the lowest-CM product, and lay it out in a report the reader can follow. One clear, complete table beats a correct answer with no workings.
  • Tax anti-avoidance depth (Tasks 2 & 6). Learn to fully explain the adverse outcome, not just name the section — s8F → interest non-deductible + deemed dividend in specie; the control test → PAYE.
  • Financial-reporting precision (Tasks 1 & 7). The compound-instrument split, and the "small" GL items (labelling-machine sign, import-duty refundability, transport-to-customers).
  • Answer the task completely (Task 4). When two things are asked (the six attributes and other King IV principles), do both, and apply — don't just state.

The "seven deadly APC sins" I need to avoid

  • Dumping everything I know instead of applying to the task (risk on Tasks 2 & 3).
  • Failing to answer the task completely (Task 4's two parts; Task 6's three components).
  • Including fundamental errors (Task 5 calc slips; Task 1 interest at the wrong rate).
  • Not doing my homework deeply enough on the harder triggers (tax, compound instruments).
Action plan before the real exam: (1) drill the CVP/relevant-costing layout until full workings are automatic; (2) build one-pagers on s8F, s19, s24J/WHT and the employees'-tax control test; (3) practise the IFRS 9 modification vs IAS 32 compound-instrument split; (4) always check "have I answered every part of the task, and applied it to the scenario?" — that single habit would lift Tasks 4, 6 and 7 from BC to C.
