NPV — Build the correct base, then critique it

IAC 2026 · MAF

Include in cash flows ✓

  • Incremental CFs only (with-vs-without)
  • After-tax operating flows
  • Tax shield = wear-&-tear/capital allowance × tax rate
  • Working capital outflow when it rises; recovered at the end
  • Opportunity cost (lost contribution / market rental)
  • Residual/scrap value & its tax (recoupment/loss)

Exclude / strip out ✗

  • Sunk costs (already spent: feasibility, past R&D)
  • Allocated head-office overheads (non-incremental)
  • Depreciation (non-cash — only its tax effect counts)
  • Financing flows: interest, loan capital, dividends (in the rate, not the CFs)

Consistency rules

  • Nominal CFs ↔ nominal (money) rate
  • Real CFs ↔ real rate — never mix
  • (1+nominal)=(1+real)(1+inflation)
  • Inflate each line at its own rate
  • Discount rate = post-tax WACC (or project Ke)
NPVNet Present Value

Timing conventions

  • Capital outflow at t 0
  • Operating flows usually year-end (t1…tn)
  • WC invested at start of the year it supports (t0)
  • Tax: same year or lagged 1 year — check the scenario
  • Discount with the right year factor

Decision & compare to…

  • NPV > 0 → accept (adds value)
  • IRR vs cost of capital (accept if IRR > WACC)
  • Sensitivity — % change to breakeven NPV
  • Scenario best/worst case
  • Payback / discounted payback (risk & liquidity)
  • Industry & historical benchmarks

Critique red flags 🔎

  • Wrong/too-low discount rate (used Ke not WACC, ignored risk)
  • Sunk or allocated costs left in
  • Depreciation deducted as a cash flow
  • WC ignored or never recovered
  • Tax omitted / no lag / no shield
  • Inflation inconsistent
  • Forecast too long; optimistic-bias growth
  • Terminal value dominates & unjustified

Company / Equity Valuation — methods & how to critique each

IAC 2026 · MAF

DCF — FCFF / FCFE

  • FCFF = EBIT(1−t) + dep − capex − ΔWC → discount at WACC → enterprise value → less net debt = equity
  • FCFE = FCFF − after-tax interest + net borrowing → discount at Ke → equity directly
  • Match the flow to the rate (FCFF↔WACC, FCFE↔Ke)

Terminal value

  • Gordon: TVₙ = CFₙ(1+g)/(r−g)
  • Discount TV back to t0
  • g must be < r & ≤ long-run GDP
  • Often the bulk of value → test it hard

Multiples (relative)

  • P/E × earnings (equity value)
  • EV/EBITDA → EV, then − net debt
  • P/B × net assets
  • Use comparable listed peers; adjust for size/risk/growth & non-recurring items
VALUEWhat is the business worth?

Net asset value (NAV)

  • Assets − liabilities at fair value (not book)
  • Add intangibles/contingents; mark to market
  • Floor value / asset-heavy or wind-up cases
  • Ignores going-concern earnings power

Dividend growth (DDM)

  • Ve = D₁/(Ke − g)
  • Best for stable dividend payers, minority stake
  • Very sensitive to Ke and g

Pick the method

  • Control stake / forecasts available → DCF
  • Quick / peers exist → multiples
  • Asset-rich / loss-making → NAV
  • Stable payer / minority → DDM
  • Triangulate & give a range
