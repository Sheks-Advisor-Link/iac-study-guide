IAC Exam Walkthroughs — how to answer, step by step

June 2025 (Ubud · SSC · Reprop) & January 2026 (Mzansi · Lexi · IR) · each part: Asked → Steps → Key points · T. Van Heerden
Universal method for every part: ① read the required & the marks (≈1 mark/point, so make that many distinct points) → ② identify the standard/section → ③ state the rule → ④ apply to the scenario facts/numbers → ⑤ conclude. Calcs: use a vertical pro-forma & show workings (own-figure marks). Discussions: a heading per point, both sides, then conclude.
JUNE 2025

Paper 1 — "Ubud" (UC: logistics app start-up) · 120

(a)(i) IAS 38 — date to recognise the CargoPlat™ app ACC 8

Asked: from which date an internally-generated intangible should have been recognised.

  1. Split spend into research (expense) vs development (capitalise).
  2. Capitalise from the date all 6 "PIRATE" criteria are met (Probable benefits · Intention · Resources · Ability to use/sell · Technical feasibility · Expenditure measurable).
  3. Point to the scenario date where the last criterion is satisfied → recognise from there; everything before = expense.
Key: can't reinstate previously-expensed research; amortise once available for use.
(a)(ii) Prior-period error note (IAS 8) ACC 8

Asked: prepare the prior-period error note extract for FY2024.

  1. It's a prior-period error → retrospective restatement.
  2. Show each affected line: "as previously stated → correction → restated" for the comparative.
  3. Restate opening retained earnings; note the nature of the error.
(b)(i) Lease — low-value/short-term exemption (IFRS 16) ACC 4

Asked: could UC elect the recognition exemption?

  1. Test low-value (value when new; benefit on its own; not dependent on/interrelated with other assets) or short-term (≤12 months).
  2. Apply the facts → conclude whether the exemption was validly elected.
(b)(ii) Lease — correcting journals (IFRS 16) ACC 12

Asked: journals to correct the lease at 1 Jan 2024 (assume no exemption).

  1. Recompute lease liability = PV of payments (implicit rate / IBR).
  2. ROU asset = liability + IDC + prepayments + dismantling − incentives.
  3. Journals: Dr ROU / Cr liability; reverse the wrong treatment; raise depreciation & interest.
(c) Audit vs independent review (Companies Act) AUD 11

Asked: must UC be audited, or is an independent review enough?

  1. Apply s30 + Reg 28/26: public/SOC? holds assets >R5m in fiduciary capacity? PIS ≥350? PIS 100–349 & internally compiled?
  2. Compute the public interest score from the facts; conclude audit vs review (or exempt if all shareholders are directors).
(d) Ethics — pre-engagement / acceptance AUD 12

Asked: ethical requirements at pre-engagement for the UC audit.

  1. Acceptance checks: client integrity, firm competence & resources, independence (CPC threats), predecessor auditor, engagement terms.
  2. For each scenario fact → name the threat & the fundamental principle; conclude on acceptance.
(e) Substantive tests of detail — lease liability AUD 15

Asked: substantive ToD on initial, subsequent & measurement of the lease liability (FY23 & FY24).

  1. Open with general (agree to contract, recompute, MRL).
  2. By assertion: recalculate the PV (agree rate, term, payments to the lease); agree opening to PY; recompute interest & the closing balance; inspect for modifications.
(f) Ratio & financial analysis (incl. unit economics) MAF 30

Asked: calculate profitability & liquidity ratios (+ unit economics) and analyse.

  1. Calculate the ratios; compare to prior year/budget/industry.
  2. For a start-up add unit economics (CAC, LTV, contribution per user, churn).
  3. Interpret: explain why each moved & the business implication; flag going-concern signs.
(g) Criticise the free-cash-flow valuation MAF 20

Asked: criticise Bongani's FCF valuation (don't re-perform).

  1. Attack the assumptions: forecast period too short, optimistic growth, discount rate not risk-adjusted, terminal value/growth, working-capital & capex ignored.
  2. Attack method: FCFF vs FCFE mismatch with the discount rate; tax; non-operating items.
Key: one valid criticism = one mark — list many, each tied to the valuation's facts.

Paper 2 — "SSC" (manufacturer; automation, acquisition of PED) · 120

(a) Capital vs revenue — robotic automation (s11(a)) TAX 7

Asked: are the automation amounts capital in nature for s11(a)?

  1. Onus on taxpayer (s102 TAA).
  2. Apply the capital-vs-revenue tests (enduring benefit / income-producing structure vs operations — New State Areas/BP).
  3. Conclude deductible or capital (→ allowance asset instead).
(b) Corrected taxable income TAX 45

Asked: recompute SSC's taxable income from PBT R29,310k.

  1. Start at PBT; add/less each adjustment on a separate line with a reason (even nil-effect items).
  2. Watch: income received in advance (earlier of receipt/accrual) + matching s24C allowance; capital allowances vs depreciation; non-deductibles; recoupments.
Key: reasons earn marks; show the s24C allowance against amounts received in advance.
(c) Report — 9 business risks + mitigations (digital strategy) MAF/AUD 20

Asked: report describing 9 digital-strategy business risks & a mitigation for each.

  1. Report format (to/from/heading).
  2. For each risk: state it from the scenario (cyber, data, system failure, skills, change, vendor lock-in, etc.) → give a practical mitigation (control).
(d) Controls over the bot's journal entries AUD 6

Asked: controls over journals recorded by the bot.

  1. Standard journal controls: training, segregation of duties, authorisation/review, validation rules, access controls, audit trail/logging, reconciliations — each tied to validity/accuracy/completeness.
(e) Acquisition date of PED (IFRS 3) ACC 7

Asked: do you agree 1 June 2024 is the acquisition date?

  1. Acquisition date = the date the acquirer obtains control (usually when consideration passes & it can direct the relevant activities).
  2. Apply the facts; state the correct date if you disagree.
(f) Goodwill / gain on bargain purchase (IFRS 3) ACC 35

Asked: calculate goodwill/bargain purchase on the PED acquisition.

  1. Goodwill = consideration + NCI + previously-held interest − FV of net identifiable assets.
  2. Fair-value the identifiable assets & liabilities (incl. deferred tax on FV adjustments); measure NCI (FV or proportionate).
  3. Negative → gain on bargain purchase (re-check first).

Paper 3 — "Reprop" (property; HOA; municipal billing) · 120

Part I (a) Auditor-appointment matters AUD 13

Asked: matters JEM Inc. should consider before accepting the Reprop audit.

  1. Acceptance: integrity, competence, independence (threats), predecessor, resources/deadline, fees, any RI history → conclude accept/decline.
Part I (b) Contingent liability — RoMM + substantive ToD AUD 19

Asked: (i) RoMM at assertion level for the contingent liability; (ii) substantive ToD (existence, accuracy/valuation, presentation).

  1. (i) State the assertion + risk + why (estimation/completeness).
  2. (ii) Per assertion: enquire of legal counsel, inspect correspondence/calculations, recompute, assess IAS 37 disclosure.
Part I (c) Ethical concerns — municipal underbilling AUD 6

Asked: ethical concerns re the underbilling decision (not CPC/IRBA codes).

  1. General ethics: honesty/integrity, fairness, legality, transparency, stakeholder harm; is it right vs merely legal?
Part I (d) Companies Act concerns AUD 14

Asked: concerns re Spark-HOA's Companies Act compliance (excl. s45 & audit-committee composition).

  1. Test directors' duties (s75/76/77), reckless trading (s22), solvency & liquidity (s4/46), record-keeping, MOI → state each breach & effect.
Part I (e) Carol's professional responsibility AUD 12

Asked: Carol's responsibility re info discovered at/after the AGM.

  1. NOCLAR steps: understand → discuss/escalate → request action → assess → consider disclosure/withdrawal → document; consider RI (APA s45) if she's the auditor.
Part II (f) Board decisions vs strategy MAF 9

Asked: do the board's decisions align with strategic objectives?

  1. List each objective → assess each decision against it → conclude aligned/not + why.
Part II (g) NPV — solar & water project MAF 21

Asked: NPV analysis & conclude whether to undertake the project.

  1. Set out incremental after-tax cash flows (year-end); ignore financing & homeowner savings as instructed.
  2. Discount → NPV; conclude (the model NPV was negative → special levy insufficient → reject unless levy increased).
Part II (h) Performance forecast — non-financial MAF 10

Asked: evaluate the forecast vs sustainability/environmental/strategic goals (non-financial KPIs).

  1. Pick relevant non-financial KPIs (energy independence, water security, carbon, resident satisfaction) → assess whether the forecast supports each goal.
Part II (i) Report — financing options MAF 16

Asked: report advising on the best financing option for the solar/water solution.

  1. Report format; compare options on cost, gearing/risk, covenants (sustainability-linked terms), flexibility, repayment → recommend.
JANUARY 2026

Paper 1 — "Mzansi" (MT retailer; inventory; Inala group) · 120

(a) RoMM for inventory AUD 16

Asked: effect of section-2 info on the RoMM for inventory.

  1. For each fact → assertion at risk + direction (over/understatement) + why; inventory is largest asset → valuation/NRV/obsolescence the big risks; conclude RoMM high.
(b) Key controls (clearance-sale data) AUD 19

Asked: controls for accuracy/completeness of data & clearance pricing.

  1. Per process: sequential numbering & sequence review, authorisation of markdowns, system validation, segregation, reconciliations — tie to accuracy/completeness.
(c) Substantive procedures + CAATs — obsolescence allowance AUD 10

Asked: ToD for the inventory obsolescence allowance.

  1. General (schedule, recompute, agree GL); GAS exception reports (items past clearance date, marked down); inventory-turnover & allowance-% analytics; cost vs NRV; subsequent sales.
(d) Going concern — action plan AUD 19

Asked: audit procedures on management's action plan.

  1. ISA 570: inspect minutes (approval), assess each action's feasibility & likelihood, agree to support/forecast, recompute, MRL.
(e) IFRS — pledged inventory & loan ACC 12

Asked: evaluate the treatment of pledged inventory & the loan.

  1. Control of inventory (direct use + benefits) — pledging is only security, control unchanged → still an asset; loan = financial liability (IFRS 9) + disclose the pledge.
(f) Accounting-authority steps (PFMA / public entity) AUD 10

Asked: steps after media criticism of the AID–Inala agreement.

  1. Before/ during/ after an investigation: inform the minister, scope it, preserve evidence, investigate, act & report — accountability & transparency.
(g) Journals — Louw Investments bond (IFRS 9) ACC 12

Asked: FY2025 journals for the bond (separate AFS).

  1. Classify (amortised cost), initial at fair value, effective-interest method; Dr Investment/Bank, recognise interest income, receipts.
(h) Group profit on disposal — loss of control (IFRS 10) ACC 22

Asked: group profit/loss on disposal of the MT shares.

  1. Compute net asset value at disposal + goodwill + NCI.
  2. Gain/loss = (consideration 90m + FV of retained interest) − (net assets + goodwill − NCI) + reclassified OCI (FV reserve net of the ECL allowance).

Paper 2 — "Lexi" (individual; CreativeLexi; financing) · 120

(a) Gross income — the trip (case law) TAX 7

Asked: income-tax implications of the all-expenses trip.

  1. IRAC: "amount in cash or otherwise" (Lategan/Brummeria) → trip MV R20k for services (quid pro quo) → par (c) services → include R20k; onus s102.
(b) Lexi's normal tax payable TAX 27

Asked: calculate her 2025 normal tax.

  1. Framework: GI → exempt → deductions → taxable → table → rebates → credits; give NIL reasons.
  2. Watch: provident FB, laptop nil, cell allowance, s7B leave, s10(1)(o)(ii) not exempt (<183 days/DTA), interest-free study loan nil, s11F, medical credits.
(c) Employees' tax — PSP TAX 7

Asked: must Hebony withhold PAYE on the R121 500?

  1. Test the personal-service-provider definition (4th Sch): company, connected person renders the service, would be an employee but for the company, <3 unconnected employees → if PSP, withhold PAYE.
(d) Interest deduction — s24J TAX 9

Asked: tax implications of R68,1m interest to Africa Holdings.

  1. s24J: loan = instrument, Holdings = issuer → YTM accrual; then check connected-party limitation (s23M/N) → conclude deductible amount.
(e) IRR — which loan offer MAF 25

Asked: by IRR, which loan is most cost-effective.

  1. Per loan: lay out after-tax cash flows (capital in, interest less tax shield via s24J, capital repayment/FV) → COMP IRR.
  2. Lower effective cost = better (Loan 1 IRR ≈ 10,22%).
(f) Advise on the loan offer MAF 12

Asked: which offer should MGT accept (quant + qual).

  1. Quant (lower IRR) + qual: fixed vs variable rate, covenants/security, repayment profile, flexibility, risk → recommend.
(g) Pre-acquisition factors (social-media KPIs) MAF 11

Asked: factors before acquiring CreativeLexi (from the KPI report).

  1. Assess the report's scope/reliability + the KPIs (followers, engagement, growth, conversion), sustainability, due diligence, integration & key-person reliance.
(h) Ethics — Arno (SAICA Code) AUD 14

Asked: ethical concerns with Arno's conduct (no safeguards/level).

  1. Per fact: name the threat (dual role → self-interest/objectivity, etc.) + the fundamental principle breached.
(i) Ethics — Lexi as CEO AUD 8

Asked: her ethical conduct re responding to the allegations (no Companies Act/King).

  1. Integrity, honesty, transparency, responsibility, accountability — did she mislead/deflect? Conclude on ethical leadership.

Paper 3 — "IR" (retailer; loyalty programme; LC stores) · 120

Part I (a) King IV — governance & ethical leadership AUD 12

Asked: governance/ethical-leadership concerns from the CEO's actions (King IV).

  1. Map each action to a King IV principle (P1 ethical leadership, P2 ethics, P6/P8 board/committees) → state the breach & recommended practice.
Part I (b) Strategic considerations — LC stores MAF 16

Asked: critically evaluate the strategy behind investing in LC stores.

  1. Strategic fit, market/competition, returns vs risk, capacity, opportunity cost, alternatives → conclude.
Part I (c) Memo to the board (calcs for Group B) MAF/ACC 36

Asked: memorandum with insights across LC stores; calculations for Group B.

  1. Memo format; perform the Group B calcs (profitability/contribution/relevant figures) → interpret across all LC groups → recommend.
Part I (d) Close Group C — relevant costing MAF 17

Asked: evaluate closing Group C stores: (i) relevant-costing impact; (ii) discuss impact on stakeholders.

  1. (i) Compare lost contribution to fixed costs saved (ignore sunk/allocated) → net effect on FY2026.
  2. (ii) Qualitative: staff, customers, reputation, knock-on, reversibility.
Part II (e) Loyalty programme (IFRS 15) ACC 17

Asked: account for the loyalty programme; (ii) change if Group C closed.

  1. Points = material right → separate performance obligation; allocate price on relative stand-alone selling price (estimate SSP × redemption probability) → defer as contract liability → revenue on redemption/expiry.
  2. (ii) reassess breakage if stores close.
Part II (f) Inventory write-down journal (IAS 2) ACC 10

Asked: evaluate the accuracy of the write-down journal + correct it.

  1. Recompute lower of cost vs NRV → compare to the journal → identify the error → correcting journal.
Part II (g) Substantive procedures — contract liability AUD 12

Asked: ToD for valuation/allocation/accuracy of the loyalty contract liability.

  1. Recompute the SSP allocation; test the redemption-rate assumption vs history; agree points data (CAATs); cut-off; subsequent redemptions.
