Part 2 — Trigger mind map (exam focus)

COLD PRESS NATIONAPC 2023 — main triggers & sub-triggers
Central = the company  ·  each box = a main trigger  ·  bullets = the sub-triggers to focus on for the exam

1 · Onverwacht acquisition (FR/Audit)

  • Business combination vs asset acquisition (IFRS 3 + concentration test)
  • Fair values at acquisition & goodwill
  • First-time consolidation of the farm
  • First-time audit: opening balances (ISA 510), risk (315), expert (500/620)
  • Vertical integration of fruit supply

2 · Parent loan – R12m (FR/Tax/Strategy)

  • IFRS 9: initial fair value / day-1 difference; amortised cost
  • Restructuring: modification vs extinguishment; convertible (IAS 32 compound)
  • Tax: s24J deduction, s31 transfer pricing, s23M, 15% WHT, DTA
  • "Unintended" tax: s8F hybrid debt, s19/para 12A debt benefit
  • Strategic reasons to restructure

3 · Agricultural assets (FR/Audit)

  • Trees = bearer plants → IAS 16 (cost/reval, depreciate)
  • Growing apples = biological assets → IAS 41 at FVLCS, changes to P/L
  • Harvest → agricultural produce at FVLCS → then IAS 2 inventory
  • Orchard land = IAS 16; input costs expensed
  • Value-creation / enterprise-value story; audit of produce

4 · Inventory (FR/Audit)

  • IAS 2 cost: fixed overhead on normal capacity
  • Three fruit sources (buy / own farm / import) — consistent costing
  • Lower of cost & NRV; batch / flash-freeze
  • Prior-period errors → IAS 8 restatement
  • Disclosure (policy + judgements); audit of inventory

5 · Spare capacity / new products (MDMC)

  • CVP: contribution margin % and operating profit margin %
  • Fixed vs variable cost split; per product & whole company
  • Relevant / incremental costing (spare capacity)
  • Product options (e.g. ginger shots, blend) & which to cut
  • Strategic fit & risks of new lines

6 · Marketing strategy (Strategy/Tax/Ethics)

  • Evaluate the 5 growth strategies
  • Tax deductibility of marketing spend (s11(a))
  • Ethics of relying on ChatGPT / AI — scepticism, verify
  • Influencer vs affiliate: employees' tax (control test), VAT

7 · Production process & controls (Controls)

  • Integration of the farm into the supply chain (internal transfers)
  • Internal controls & process risks; cold chain
  • Organic claim on packaging (links to ethics/greenwashing)

8 · Overarching themes (All)

  • 2nd year of full IFRS (watch IAS 8 / first-time items)
  • MegaDrinks group reporting & deadlines
  • Ethics: honesty of claims; newly-qualified CA competence
  • Financial-statement analysis of the mgmt accounts
