How to use: Part 1 = every rule with its key numbers & mechanism. Part 2 = the principle each worked example teaches. Part 3 = the case-law principles + the exact key words that earn marks in discussion answers. ⚠ Confirm rand thresholds on the exam rate card.

Part 1 · Every tax rule, in simple terms (with the detail)

Gross income (s1) & residence

  • Resident = ordinarily resident (your "real home" — the place you'd naturally return to) or the physical-presence day test (91 days this yr + 91 avg over 5 yrs + 915 total) → taxed worldwide. Non-resident → only SA-source.
  • "Total amount, in cash or otherwise" = money or anything with an ascertainable money value; valued at the date you become entitled.
  • "Received by" = for your own benefit (deposits still count); "accrued to" = unconditionally entitled; taxed at the earlier of the two. Amounts are not discounted to present value.
  • Capital receipts excluded — judged on intention / scheme of profit-making (the income-producing "tree" vs its "fruit"; a change of intention). Onus that it's capital is on the taxpayer (s102).
  • Special inclusions (par a–n) pull amounts in even if capital: annuities, alimony, services/restraint (cA/cB), retirement lump sums (e), recoupments (n), lease premiums (g), leasehold improvements (h).

Exempt income (s10) & s24C

  • Local dividends exempt for normal tax (s10(1)(k)) — but 20% dividends tax. Interest exemption R23 800 (<65) / R34 500 (65+).
  • Foreign employment s10(1)(o)(ii): exempt up to R1.25m if >183 days (incl. 60 continuous) abroad. Foreign dividends partly exempt (s10B; full if ≥10% holding).
  • s24C allowance: income received in advance under a contract that funds future deductible costs under the same contract → defer the matching portion; reverses next year.

Deductions — s11(a) + s23

  • Positive test (s11(a)): ① a trade; ② expenditure/loss; ③ actually incurred = unconditional legal liability; ④ in production of income = closely connected to income-earning operations; ⑤ in the year; ⑥ not capital (fixed vs floating capital — income-producing structure vs operations).
  • Negative test (s23): private/domestic & home office (a),(b); not-for-trade (g); the tax itself (d); reserves/provisions; bribes/fines (o).
  • s23H prepaid: only the portion enjoyed this year — unless total prepaid ≤R100k or goods/services delivered within 6 months of year-end.
  • s11A pre-trade (ring-fenced); s11(c) legal (non-capital, trade); s11(d) repairs; s11(i)/(j) bad/doubtful debts; s11(l)/(m) fund contributions / annuities.

Trading stock (s22)

  • Closing stock added to income at lower of cost or NRV; opening stock deducted; purchases deducted (s11(a)). Cost = price + costs to bring to present location & condition.
  • s22(4) free/inherited stock → market value in. s22(8) stock to private use / donated / dividend in specie → deemed recovery at cost or MV. s23F denies the deduction until stock disposed / income received.

Capital allowances

  • s11(e) movables: straight-line over IN47 life, apportioned part-year; <R7 000 items full write-off.
  • s12C manufacturing plant: 40/20/20/20 new, 20%×5 used — full year, no apportion; cost includes installation; later moving costs over remaining life.
  • s12E SBC 100% (mfg) / 50-30-20. s13 industrial 5%; s13quin commercial 5%; s13sex residential 5% (10% low-cost), need ≥5 units; 55% of price if you buy (not build) part of a building.
  • s12B/12BA renewables 100% / 125% (window). s11(f) lease premium over lease term (max 25 yrs); s11(g) improvements over months left from completion; s12N lessee deemed owner of improvements.

Recoupments & scrapping

  • s8(4) recoupment on disposal = lower(proceeds, cost) − tax value; included in income; capped at allowances claimed (excess over cost → CGT).
  • s11(o) scrapping loss if proceeds < tax value (asset life ≤10 yrs). s24M/s20B for proceeds not determinable / not yet accrued.

CGT (8th Schedule, via s26A)

  • Gain = proceeds (par 35) − base cost (par 20). Base cost = cost + transfer duty + improvements + advertising + agent's commission (exclude repairs); pre-2001 → valuation/TAB/20% rule.
  • Net gains − annual exclusion R40k (R300k in death year) × inclusion 40% indiv / 80% co. Effective max: 18% / 21.6% / trust 36%.
  • Deemed disposals at MV: donation, death (s9HA), emigration (s9H), dividend in specie, asset→private use, connected persons. Roll-overs: spouse (s9HB), insurance/replacement (par 65/66).
  • Primary residence: first R2m of gain free (apportion for >2ha/business use). Personal-use-asset losses disregarded (par 53). s9C: equity shares held ≥3 yrs = capital.

VAT (15%, fraction 15/115)

  • Output − input = VAT. Register if 12-month taxable supplies >R1m (voluntary >R50k). Zero-rated (exports, basic foods, fuel, going concern) keep input; exempt (interest, residential rent, transport, education) get no input.
  • Time of supply (s9): earlier of invoice/payment; connected persons = delivery/performance; fixed property (s9(3)(d)) = earlier of registration/payment.
  • Imported goods: (customs value + 10% + duties) × 15%. Imported services: VAT on the non-taxable-use %. Input denied (s17(2)): entertainment, motor cars, club subs. Adjustments: change-in-use (s18), bad debts (s22).

Individuals — allowances

  • Travel (s8(1)(b)): included, reduce by business portion — deemed cost (fixed+fuel+maintenance tables ÷ total km × business km) or actual; logbook required; 80% through PAYE (20% if >80% business).
  • Subsistence (s8(1)(c)): away ≥1 night → reduce by deemed daily amounts (meals+incidental / incidental only) or actual.
  • Reimbursive travel tax-free up to the prescribed rate/km if no other allowance.

Fringe benefits (7th Schedule)

  • 2(a) asset cheap/free = MV (cost if stock) − paid. 2(b) company car = 3.5%/mth of determined value (3.25% with maintenance plan), reduce for business km & employee-borne costs.
  • 2(d) accommodation by formula (A−B)×C/100×D/12. 2(f) low-interest loan = (official − charged) × loan × period; official = repo+1%. 2(l) employer fund contributions taxable but deductible via s11F.

Retirement & medical

  • s11F: deduct contributions up to 27.5% of the higher of remuneration / taxable income (incl. TCG), capped R350k; excess carries forward.
  • s6A medical credit (fixed monthly per member/dependant — a rebate). s6B additional credit (25% / 33⅓% of qualifying out-of-pocket above the threshold).

Employees' & provisional tax

  • PAYE on "remuneration" (4th Sch); independent contractors excluded (control/supervision; premises + ≥3 staff exclusion). Labour brokers need an exemption certificate; PSPs taxed at company rate.
  • Provisional: 1st (6 mths) = 50% of estimate; 2nd (year-end) = full estimate — within 80% if TI>R1m else par-20 penalty; use "basic amount" (last assessment +8%/yr if >18 mths old) if low; 3rd = voluntary top-up.

Lump sums & severance

  • Retirement / death / retrenchment / severance → retirement table (first ~R550k at 0%); resignation → harsher withdrawal table (first R27 500 at 0%).
  • Cumulative: tax all lumps to date, less tax on prior lumps. Subtract non-deducted contributions first; leftover exempts annuity income (s10C). Severance needs ≥55 / ill-health / retrenchment.

Dividends, CTC & companies

  • Dividends tax 20% on the beneficial owner; the company pays it on dividends in specie and deemed dividends (cheap shareholder loans). SA-resident company / fund / PBO owners exempt (s64F, with declaration).
  • CTC = consideration for shares issued; returning CTC is not a dividend (reduces base cost). Company rate 27%; s20 assessed-loss set-off capped at higher of R1m / 80% of TI; s20A ring-fences suspect trades.
  • Corporate rules: s42 asset-for-share, s45 intra-group (≥70%), s46 unbundling → roll-over (also covers transfer duty/STT/VAT; 6-yr de-grouping charge).

Interest, forex & foreign

  • s24J: interest on an "instrument" spread on a yield-to-maturity basis (overrides cash; s23B gives it priority over s11(a)). s24O interest to buy operating-company shares deductible; s8F/8FA hybrid interest = dividend in specie.
  • s25D translate forex at spot/average; s24I monetary items retranslated at year-end (realised + unrealised); s24I(7) defers pre-production differences. s6quat foreign tax rebate (capped); s9D CFC; s31 transfer pricing (arm's length + 20% secondary).

Estate duty & donations

  • Estate duty: (property s3(2) + deemed property s3(3): life policies, accrual claim − deductions s4: debts, admin, spouse s4(q), PBO − s4A abatement R3.5m, portable to R7m) × 20% (25% >R30m).
  • Donations tax 20% (25% >R30m cumulative) on MV of gratuitous disposals; R100k annual exemption (natural persons); spouse / PBO / maintenance / 6-month-return exempt. s58 deemed donation for inadequate consideration; s7 attributes diverted income back to the donor.

Trusts & deceased estates

  • Conduit (s25B / par 80): income/gains keep their nature and are taxed in the vested beneficiary; retained & un-attributed amounts taxed in the trust (45%).
  • Attribution (s7/par 68–73): minor child (s7(3)), spouse (s7(2)), non-resident (s7(8)), retained (s7(5)) → back to donor. s7C: interest-free loan to a trust = deemed annual donation. s9HA death = MV disposal (spouse roll-over s9HB).

TAA & GAAR

  • s102 onus on taxpayer; 3-year prescription (5 self-assessment) unless fraud/non-disclosure; s104 objection → appeal → ADR/Tax Board/Tax Court; s164 pay-now-argue-later; s222 understatement penalty (0–200%); VDP relief.
  • GAAR (s80A): arrangement + sole/main purpose = tax benefit + a tainted element (abnormality / no commercial substance / misuse). Apply specific anti-avoidance first.

Part 2 · One principle from every worked example (with the detail)

Gross income — illustrative examples

  • Free use of money: an interest-free loan is GI = interest saved (prime × loan × period). 10% × R10m × 7/12 = R583 333; a 5% loan → only the 5% shortfall.
  • Payment in kind: shares/goods received as payment are valued at market value on the date of entitlement, not the (lower) contract value.
  • Notional income: what you could have earned (cash hidden under a floor) is never taxed — no receipt/accrual.
  • Agent money: a R60k rent collection where you keep 10% → only R6 000 is GI; the R54k passed on isn't yours. Never include R60k then deduct R54k.
  • Overpayment: a duplicate receipt isn't GI until you're unconditionally entitled (repayable for 5 yrs); may be recouped if never repaid.
  • Illegal income (e.g. drug sales) is still fully taxable. No discounting: R15m receivable in 11 months is GI at the full R15m, not its PV.

Deductions

  • s11A: pre-trade rates of R75k shelter only the R35k same-trade income; the R40k balance carries forward — it can't reduce other (service) income.
  • s23H: office & security prepayments of R132 500 (>R100k, >6 months) are not deductible now; stationery (delivered within 6 months) is fully deductible despite being prepaid.
  • s11(c): legal fees to enforce a loss-of-profits insurance claim are deductible (non-capital, protects trade income) — and the payout is itself taxable.
  • s11(l)/(m): employer pension/RA contributions deductible (s11(l)); annuities to staff who retired due to old age fully deductible (s11(m)).

Trading stock & foreign exchange (Corporate Tax)

  • s23F: no deduction for stock purchased but not yet sold / where the related income hasn't been received.
  • Cost of stock: includes customs & costs to bring it to present location/condition — not just the invoice price.
  • Debt benefit on stock: a waived loan that funded trading stock reduces the cost of that stock (s19), not a recoupment.
  • s22(4): stock acquired for no consideration enters at market value (and the same MV is the deemed cost).
  • s22(8): stock taken for private use or given as a dividend in specie triggers a deemed recovery at cost/MV.
  • s25D: a foreign-currency transaction is translated to rand at the spot rate on transaction date.
  • s24I: forex debtors/creditors/loans are retranslated at year-end — recognise both realised and unrealised gains/losses (transaction date → year-end → realisation date).
  • s24I(7): exchange differences on a capital asset bought before it's brought into use are deferred until the asset is in use.

Capital allowances (Corporate Tax)

  • s11(e): R50k vehicle, used 1 Sep, 4-yr life → Y1 50 000 × ¼ × 4/12 = 4 167; Y2 12 500 (apportion Y1 only).
  • s12C: new machine R2m + R50k install → ×40% = 820 000 full year; used machine → 20%; later moving costs over remaining life.
  • s13sex: flats bought → 500k × 5% × 5 × 55% = 68 750; low-cost houses built → 280k × 10% × 5 = 140 000.
  • Lease (s11(f)/(g)/s13): premium spread over the lease; obligated improvement over months left from completion; spend above the obligation gets the normal 5% building allowance.
  • s12N: a lessee improving (government) land is deemed the owner → claims the s13-type allowance on the improvement.
  • IP (s11(gC)): patents/designs get an allowance over their life; trademarks get nothing.

Capital gains tax (CGT lecture — 23 examples)

  • CGT-1 residence: residents = worldwide gains; non-residents only on SA immovable property & permanent-establishment assets.
  • CGT-2 intention: a private car sold without a profit-scheme is capital — and a personal-use asset, so the gain is disregarded.
  • CGT-3/4 mechanics: sum gains & losses, the annual exclusion reduces both, then apply the inclusion rate.
  • CGT-5 emigration (s9H): ceasing residence = deemed disposal at MV of all assets except SA immovable property & cash.
  • CGT-6 immigration: on becoming resident, base cost of foreign assets is reset to market value.
  • CGT-7 base cost: cost R1m + transfer duty 50k + jacuzzi (improvement) 15k + advertising 30k + 5% agent commission — repairs excluded.
  • CGT-8 allowance asset: remove the recoupment from proceeds and reduce base cost by allowances; the recoupment can never exceed past allowances.
  • CGT-9: donations tax paid is added (proportionally) to the donee's base cost.
  • CGT-10 proceeds: reduce proceeds by any portion already taxed in gross income — no double tax.
  • CGT-12/13 primary residence: first R2m of the gain excluded; apportion for land >2ha or business/letting use.
  • CGT-14 to personal use: deemed disposal at MV; a loss on a personal-use asset is disregarded (par 53).
  • CGT-15 par 65: insurance/involuntary proceeds qualify for roll-over; insurance is limited to original cost and the gain can be spread over the replacement asset.
  • CGT-16 connected persons: a non-arm's-length deal is treated as happening at market value.
  • CGT-17 death (s9HA): deemed disposal at MV at death — except assets left to a spouse, which roll over at base cost (s9HB).
  • CGT-18 s9C: equity shares held ≥3 years are deemed capital — even a share dealer's; a change to capital intention is at MV.
  • CGT-19 dividend in specie: distributing an asset is a deemed disposal at market value.
  • CGT-20 par 76B: a return of capital reduces base cost; once base cost is nil the excess is an immediate capital gain.
  • CGT-21–23 par 12A: a waived debt that funded an asset reduces that asset's base cost (allowance assets also have future allowances capped, s19(7)).

VAT (worked examples)

  • Registration: compulsory once 12-month taxable supplies exceed R1m; add all branches together (s50) — you can't split to avoid it.
  • Import goods: base = customs value + 10% + duties, then ×15% (137 600 × 15% = 20 640); full input if 100% taxable use.
  • Import services: self-account VAT only on the exempt-use portion (60 000 × 80% × 15% = 7 200).
  • Exports: zero-rated only with export proof within 90 days — otherwise standard-rated at 15%.
  • Going concern: zero-rated when the criteria are met and both parties are vendors.
  • Fixed property: output/input on a payment basis (s9(3)(d) / s16(4)) — only as the purchase price is settled.
  • Credit waiver: a loan is an exempt financial service → no VAT reverses; s22 only applies if the creditor who waived also charged the original VAT.

Individuals & fringe benefits (Worked Examples 2024)

  • Assessed loss s20A: losses from "suspect" hobby-type trades are ring-fenced if you've lost in 3 of the last 5 years (or it's a listed trade).
  • Travel allowance: reduce by business km using the deemed-cost tables or actual costs — a logbook is essential; 80% is subject to PAYE.
  • Subsistence: deemed daily amounts apply only when away from home ≥1 night.
  • Car FB: 3.5% (3.25% with a maintenance plan) of determined value per month, reduced for business use and employee-borne running costs.
  • Loan FB: benefit = (official rate − rate charged) × loan × period.
  • VAT on FB: the employer accounts output VAT (tax fraction) on the cash equivalent of the benefit.
  • s8C shares: the gain (MV − consideration) on a restricted equity instrument is taxed as remuneration on vesting, not on grant.

Employees' & provisional tax (Worked Examples 2024)

  • Independent trade: not an employee if free of control/supervision; statutory exclusion if own premises & ≥3 unconnected staff.
  • Labour broker: PAYE must be withheld unless a valid exemption certificate is produced.
  • Personal service provider: taxed at the company rate with very limited deductions; the payer withholds PAYE.
  • Basic amount: if the latest assessment is >18 months old, escalate it by 8% per year for the 2nd provisional estimate.
  • 2nd provisional: if taxable income > R1m, the estimate must be within 80% of actual or a 20% under-estimation penalty (par 20) applies.

Retirement (worked examples)

  • s11F (Zubaida): deduction = lesser of R350k and 27.5% × higher of remuneration (incl. employer-contribution FB) / taxable income; excess (R118 550) carries forward.
  • Severance: a lump sum is a severance benefit only if ≥55, ill-health, or retrenchment (not an early-resignation package under 55).
  • Withdrawal vs retirement: amounts transferred between funds are tax-free (para 6); only cash taken is taxed, on the harsher withdrawal table.
  • Cumulative (Simone): tax on all lump sums to date less tax on prior lump sums = tax on the current one (633 150 − 357 750 = 275 405).
  • s10C (Natalie): contributions never deducted first reduce the lump sum, then exempt the annuity income.

Dividends, CTC & corporate rules

  • CTC: a capitalisation issue out of profits is tainted (not CTC); only genuine paid-in capital is CTC, and returning it isn't a dividend.
  • Dividend in specie / cheap loan: the company pays the 20% (deemed dividend on a low-interest shareholder loan = official rate × loan × period).
  • s64N: a rebate for foreign dividend tax already withheld caps total tax at 20% (e.g. 7% foreign + 13% local).
  • Liquidation: split the distribution into CTC (capital, no DWT) and dividend (20% DWT); company shareholders are exempt (s64F) but still get the cash.
  • s42 asset-for-share: roll-over relief (transfer at base cost/tax value) when the requirements are met.
  • s24BA: an asset-for-share at a value mismatch creates a company capital gain / reduced base cost in the shares.
  • s103: changing shareholding mainly to use an assessed loss is struck down (anti-avoidance).

Donations, estate duty & attribution

  • Donations tax (Sam): 20% on market value after the R100k exemption (185 000 − 100 000 = 85 000 × 20% = 17 000); payable by end of the next month.
  • Exemptions: spouse (unlimited), bona-fide maintenance, PBO, and assets returned within 6 months are all exempt — the R100k general exemption is used only once.
  • s7 attribution (Sam): donating rental properties to a minor (s7(3)) and a non-resident (s7(8)) child still taxes that income in the donor — and donations tax applies too.
  • Estate duty (Mr A): (property + deemed 7 352 000 − debts/admin 172 000 − R3.5m) × 20% = 736 000.
  • Estate valuation: use death-date MV (or the actual arm's-length sale price); ignore later damage or a below-value "sweetheart" sale in the will.

Trusts

  • Conduit (Connect Trust): split income by type, then tax each slice in the vested beneficiary (keeping its nature — interest gets s10(1)(i), dividends s10(1)(k)); retained, un-donated income is taxed in the trust at 45%.
  • Attribution: a minor's income / income from a donor-funded asset is taxed in the donor (s7(3)/(5)) — but income from a different donor's asset (e.g. grandfather's) is not attributed to the father.
  • s25B(3): a beneficiary's deduction is capped at their vested income — a property loss stays trapped in the trust.
  • s7C: an interest-free / low-interest loan to a trust creates a deemed annual donation equal to the foregone interest (uses the R100k exemption).

International & withholding (s35A / s50)

  • s35A (Greg): the buyer withholds 7.5% (individual) / 10% (company) / 15% (trust) of the price as an advance against the non-resident seller's CGT (5.5m × 7.5% = 412 500); the seller still does the full CGT calc.
  • s50B (Tiaan): 15% WHT on SA-source interest to a non-resident — but interest from a bank and listed bonds is exempt (s50D); all of it is exempt from normal tax (s10(1)(h)).
  • s6quat: a rebate for foreign tax paid, limited to the SA tax attributable to that foreign income.
  • s9D / s10B: CFC net income is imputed to >50% resident shareholders; foreign dividends enjoy the participation (≥10%) or ratio exemption.

Part 3 · Case-law summary — the principle + the key words that earn marks

In a discussion answer, naming the case is a bonus; stating the principle and using the highlighted key words earns the mark. Write: "the principle in [case] is that … [key words] … applied to the facts, therefore …".
