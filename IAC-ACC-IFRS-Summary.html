IFRS Topic Summary — Classify · Recognise · Measure · Journals

Every standard in one structure · SAICA IAC 2026 · T. Van Heerden  |  consolidations & associates are in the separate "Control Interests" pack
Read every topic the same way: Classify (what box?) → Initial recognition (when?) → Initial measurement (at what amount?) → Subsequent measurement (model?) → Journals. Master that rhythm and you can attack any IFRS question. Dr = debit, Cr = credit.

Conceptual Framework — the foundation

Asset a present economic resource controlled by the entity from past events (potential to produce economic benefits).
Liability a present obligation to transfer an economic resource as a result of past events.
Recognise when it meets the definition AND provides users with relevant info that faithfully represents (and benefits > cost).
Measure historical cost OR current value (fair value, value in use, current cost). Each standard picks the basis.

1 · Non-current assets

IAS 16 — Property, Plant & Equipment

Classify tangible items held for use in production/supply/rental/admin, expected >1 period.
Recognise probable future economic benefits + cost measured reliably.
Initial meas. cost = purchase price (less trade discount) + import duties/non-refundable taxes + directly attributable costs (delivery, installation, testing, site prep) + PV of dismantling/restoration estimate + qualifying borrowing costs (IAS 23).
Subsequent Cost model (cost − accumulated depreciation − impairment) or Revaluation model (fair value at reval date − subsequent dep − impairment). Depreciate over useful life to residual value; component depreciation; review UL/residual/method annually (= change in estimate, prospective, IAS 8).

IAS 23 — Borrowing Costs

Classify interest & other costs to borrow funds.
Capitalise those directly attributable to a qualifying asset (takes a substantial time to get ready). Specific loan: actual costs less investment income on temporary surplus. General: capitalisation rate × expenditure. Start when expenditure + borrowing + activities begin; suspend during idle periods; stop when ready.
Else expense to P/L.

IAS 40 — Investment Property

Classify land/building held to earn rentals or for capital appreciation — NOT owner-occupied (IAS 16) and not for sale (IAS 2/IFRS 5).
Recognise/Initial as IAS 16 — cost + transaction costs.
Subsequent Fair value model (remeasure to FV each year, changes → P/L, no depreciation) OR Cost model (as IAS 16). Policy applies to all IP.

Cr Fair value adjustment (P/L)x
Change of useTransfer in/out (e.g. owner-occupied → IP) at the date use changes; under FV model, remeasure to FV with the IAS 16 revaluation rules up to transfer date.

IAS 38 — Intangible Assets

Classify identifiable (separable OR arising from contractual/legal rights) non-monetary asset without physical substance.
Recognise probable benefits + cost reliable. Research → expense. Development → capitalise if "PIRATE": Probable benefits · Intention to complete · Resources adequate · Ability to use/sell · Technical feasibility · Expenditure measurable. Internally generated goodwill/brands/mastheads → never recognised.
Initial meas. cost (purchased) or directly attributable development cost.
Subsequent cost or revaluation (only if an active market). Finite life → amortise over UL; indefinite → no amortisation, annual impairment test.

IAS 36 — Impairment of Assets

Scope most non-financial assets (PPE, intangibles, goodwill, investments in subs/associates).
Trigger indicators (external: market/rates/decline; internal: damage, obsolescence, worse performance) OR annual for goodwill & indefinite-life intangibles.
Measure impairment = carrying amount − recoverable amount; recoverable = higher of FV less costs of disposal & value in use (PV of future cash flows at a pre-tax discount rate).
CGU if asset doesn't generate independent cash flows, test the cash-generating unit; allocate loss to goodwill first, then pro-rata to other assets (not below their own recoverable amount).

ReversalReverse if indicators gone — but limited to depreciated historical cost, and goodwill impairment is never reversed.

2 · Inventories & assets held for sale

IAS 2 — Inventories

Classify held for sale in the ordinary course, in production for sale, or materials/supplies to be consumed.
Measure at the lower of cost and NRV. Cost = purchase + conversion (direct labour + production overheads) + costs to bring to present location/condition; formula FIFO or weighted average (no LIFO). NRV = estimated selling price − costs to complete − costs to sell.
Dr Cost of sales / write-down (P/L)x
Cr Inventory (allowance)x
Example (IAS 2)Cost R100; NRV = selling R110 − completion R5 − selling R12 = R93 → carry at R93, write down R7 to P/L. Reversal allowed if NRV recovers (limited to original write-down).

IFRS 5 — Non-current Assets Held for Sale & Discontinued Operations

Classify HFS when carrying amount will be recovered principally through sale: available for immediate sale in present condition AND sale highly probable (committed plan, active marketing at a reasonable price, expected within 12 months).
Measure at the lower of carrying amount and fair value less costs to sell; stop depreciating; present separately (current asset).
Discontinued op a component disposed of / held for sale that is a separate major line of business or geographical area → present its results separately.

3 · IFRS 16 — Leases

Classify a contract that conveys the right to control the use of an identified asset for a period in exchange for consideration (control = direct the use + obtain substantially all the economic benefits).
Recognise (lessee) at commencement, a right-of-use (ROU) asset & a lease liability — unless short-term (≤12m) or low-value (expense on a straight-line basis).
Initial meas. Liability = PV of lease payments (fixed + in-substance fixed + variable on index/rate + residual value guarantees + purchase/termination options reasonably certain) at the rate implicit in the lease (or the incremental borrowing rate). ROU asset = liability + initial direct costs + prepayments + dismantling estimate − lease incentives received.
Subsequent Liability at amortised cost (add interest, deduct payments). ROU asset at cost − depreciation − impairment, over the shorter of lease term & useful life (or UL if ownership transfers/purchase option certain).

4 · IFRS 15 — Revenue from Contracts with Customers

5 steps ① identify the contract → ② identify performance obligations (distinct goods/services) → ③ determine the transaction price → ④ allocate the price to each PO (relative stand-alone selling price) → ⑤ recognise revenue as/when each PO is satisfied.
Timing Over time if: customer simultaneously receives & consumes; OR the entity creates/enhances an asset the customer controls; OR no alternative use + enforceable right to payment for work done. Otherwise point in time (control transfers).
Price issues variable consideration (expected value / most likely + constraint); significant financing component (discount); non-cash; amounts payable to customer; principal vs agent (gross vs net).

5 · Financial instruments (IFRS 9 & IAS 32)

IFRS 9 — Financial assets & liabilities

Classify (assets) by business model + SPPI test: Amortised cost (hold to collect + cash flows are solely principal & interest); FVOCI (debt) (hold to collect and sell + SPPI); FVTPL (everything else / trading). Equity investments: default FVTPL, irrevocable election to FVOCI (gains/losses never recycled; only dividends → P/L).
Initial meas. fair value + transaction costs (but for FVTPL, transaction costs → P/L).
Subsequent per class. Amortised cost = effective-interest method. FVOCI debt: FV in OCI, but interest/ECL/FX in P/L; recycle on disposal. Impairment (ECL): 3-stage model; simplified (lifetime ECL) for trade receivables.
Liabilities amortised cost (default) or FVTPL.

IAS 32 — Presentation (liability vs equity)

Classify as a liability if there's a contractual obligation to deliver cash/another asset; as equity if no such obligation (residual interest).
Compound instrument (e.g. convertible bond): split — liability = PV of cash flows at the market rate for similar non-convertible debt; equity = residual (proceeds − liability).

6 · Provisions & liabilities (IAS 37)

Classify provision = a liability of uncertain timing/amount. Contingent liability = possible obligation / not probable / can't measure → disclose only. Contingent asset → disclose if probable; recognise only when virtually certain.
Recognise provision if ALL: ① a present obligation (legal or constructive) from a past event; ② probable outflow; ③ reliable estimate.
Measure best estimate of the expenditure (expected value for large populations; most likely for single items); discount if the time value is material.

7 · Employee benefits & share-based payments

IAS 19 — Employee Benefits

Short-term (salaries, leave, bonus) → accrue undiscounted in the period earned.
Post-employment Defined contribution → expense the contributions. Defined benefit → recognise the net defined benefit liability; service cost & net interest → P/L; remeasurements (actuarial gains/losses) → OCI (not recycled).

IFRS 2 — Share-based Payments

Equity-settled measure at the grant-date fair value of the equity instruments; expense over the vesting period; adjust for service/non-market conditions (not for market conditions). No remeasurement after grant.
Cash-settled (e.g. share appreciation rights) measure at the fair value of the liability, remeasured each reporting date through P/L until settled.

8 · IAS 12 — Income Taxes (incl. deferred tax)

Current tax tax payable on taxable profit for the year (per the Tax Act).
Deferred tax on temporary differences = carrying amount − tax base, × the tax rate. Taxable temp diff → DTL; deductible temp diff & assessed losses → DTA (only if probable future taxable profit). Use the rate expected when it reverses (e.g. 80% CGT inclusion → effective rate for capital items).
Exemptions initial-recognition exemption; tax on items in OCI/equity goes to OCI/equity (e.g. revaluation).

Example (IAS 12)Asset carrying amount R500k, tax base R350k → taxable temp diff R150k × 27% = DTL R40 500: Dr Tax expense, Cr Deferred tax liability.

9 · Foreign currency (IAS 21) & grants (IAS 20)

IAS 21 — The Effects of Changes in Foreign Exchange Rates

Initial record a foreign transaction at the spot rate on the transaction date.
Year-end monetary items (cash, receivables, payables, loans) → retranslate at the closing rate; exchange differences → P/L. Non-monetary at cost → keep historical rate; at fair value → rate when FV measured.
Foreign operation translate assets/liabilities at closing rate, income at average; differences → OCI (FCTR), recycled on disposal.

IAS 20 — Government Grants

Recognise when there's reasonable assurance the entity will comply with the conditions AND the grant will be received.
Match to the related costs (income approach). Income grant → P/L (or net against the expense). Asset grant → either deferred income (release over the asset's life) or deduct from the asset's cost.

10 · Policies, events & presentation

IAS 8 — Policies, Estimates & Errors

  • Change in accounting policy → apply retrospectively (restate comparatives).
  • Change in estimate (UL, residual, ECL) → prospective (current & future).
  • Prior-period error → retrospective restatement (restate comparatives, opening retained earnings).

IAS 10 — Events After the Reporting Period

  • Adjusting events: provide evidence of conditions at year-end → adjust the AFS (e.g. debtor insolvency, NRV evidence, court ruling).
  • Non-adjusting: arose after year-end → disclose only (e.g. fire, dividend declared, share issue). Going concern after year-end → always adjust the basis.

IAS 33 — Earnings Per Share (listed)

Basic EPS = profit attributable to ordinary shareholders ÷ weighted-average ordinary shares.
Diluted EPS adjusts for potential ordinary shares (options, convertibles) that would reduce EPS.
Exam technique for ACCFor any item: ① state the standard & classify it → ② recognition criteria (met?) → ③ initial measurement (show the cost build-up) → ④ subsequent model + this year's movement → ⑤ the journal → ⑥ deferred-tax & disclosure consequence. Always tie the number to the principle; show workings for own-figure marks.
