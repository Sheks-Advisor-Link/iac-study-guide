PAPER 1 — Ubud (UC: logistics-app start-up) · 120 marks

(a)(i) 8 marks · ACC

With reference to the development of the CargoPlat™ app, explain with reasons from which date an intangible asset should have been recognised, given that the app meets the definition of an intangible asset (IAS 38).

How to answer

  1. Split spend into research (expense) and development (may capitalise).
  2. Capitalise from the date all six development criteria are first met.
  3. Pin the date to a fact in the scenario; expense everything before it.

Model answer

  • Capitalise development only when the entity can demonstrate all of: technical feasibility · intention to complete · ability to use/sell · probable future benefits · adequate resources · expenditure measurable reliably (IAS 38.57).
  • Technical feasibility was met on 2 May 2022 when the board approved the technical details; recognise from the date the last criterion is met.
  • Earlier research/development already expensed cannot be reinstated.

(a)(ii) 8 marks · ACC

Prepare an extract of the prior-period error note in UC's FY2024 financial statements (show each affected line only; no totals; ignore tax).

How to answer

  1. It's a prior-period error → retrospective restatement of comparatives.
  2. Show each affected P/L, OCI and SFP line for 31/12/2023 & 31/12/2022.

Model answer

  • P/L: (increase) in amortisation/cost of sales — amortisation R5 010 000 ÷ 6 yrs ≈ R835 000.
  • OCI: (decrease) in revaluation surplus.
  • SFP: adjust the intangible, accumulated amortisation, revaluation surplus & retained earnings per year.

(b)(i) 4 marks · ACC

With reference to the lease of the server, discuss whether you agree that UC may have elected the recognition exemption at the commencement of the lease.

How to answer

  1. Test "low value": (a) usable on its own and (b) not highly interrelated with other assets (IFRS 16.B5); or short-term ≤12 months.

Model answer

  • The server's components only work together as one device → highly interrelated → criterion (b) fails.
  • Conclusion: not low-value → the exemption could not be elected → recognise a right-of-use asset & lease liability.

(b)(ii) 12 marks · ACC

Prepare the journal entries to correct all aspects of the lease on 1 January 2024 (assume no exemption; prior-year effects to opening balances; ignore tax).

How to answer

  1. Recompute the lease liability (PV) and the ROU asset, then journalise the correction.

  • Dr ROU asset 720 508 / Cr Lease liability / Cr Accumulated depreciation / Cr Retained earnings (opening) — then raise current-year depreciation & interest.

(g) 20 marks · MAF

Criticise the free-cash-flow valuation performed by Bongani. (Do not re-perform it; assume the arithmetic is correct.)

PAPER 2 — SSC (manufacturer; automation; acquisition of PED) · 120

(a) 7 marks · TAX

Discuss and conclude whether the robotic-process-automation amounts are capital in nature in terms of s11(a) for the year ended 31 December 2024.

How to answer

  1. Onus on taxpayer (s102); apply the capital-vs-revenue case law per item.

Model answer

  • Operations-vs-structure test (New State Areas): income-earning operations = deductible; enduring benefit/structure = capital.
  • The R1.5m bot improves the income-earning structure → capital, not deductible; assess the R350 000 automation by its purpose; conclude per item.

(b) 45 marks · TAX

Calculate SSC's corrected taxable income for the year ended 31 December 2024, starting from profit before tax of R29 310 000, with brief reasons per line (R'000).

How to answer

  1. Start at PBT; each adjustment on its own line with a reason (incl. nil-effect items).

Model answer

  • Amounts received in advance: only the amount unconditionally entitled is GI (earlier of receipt/accrual) — include R3.5m received; production cost deductible only when incurred (2025).
  • Claim the s24C allowance on income received in advance; add back the bot (capital) & claim correct capital allowances; non-deductibles; recoupments.

(c) 20 marks · MAF/AUD

Draft a report to management describing nine key business risks relating to the digital strategy, and for each, a mitigating measure. (Ignore ethics & bot-journal controls.)

How to answer

  1. Report format; pair each risk with a practical mitigation.

Model answer

  • Risks: strategy not cohesive; cyber/data security; system downtime; skills/change management; vendor lock-in; integration; cost overruns; obsolescence; reputation.
  • Mitigations: governance over strategy approval, cyber controls, BCP/DR, training, dual suppliers, phased rollout, monitoring.

(d) 6 marks · AUD

Describe the controls that should be put in place over the journal entries recorded by the bot.

Model answer

  • Training; segregation of duties (config vs operate vs oversee); user & bot access controls (least privilege); bot can't create & approve the same journal; supporting docs reviewed before posting; independent review/approval; logging; reconciliations.

(e) 7 marks · ACC

Explain whether you agree with the bot that 1 June 2024 is the acquisition date of PED (per IFRS); state the correct date if you disagree.

Model answer

  • Acquisition date = when SSC obtains control (power + variable returns + ability to use power).
  • Per PED's MOI, power = voting rights. SSC has 10% + an option over 60% → control depends on whether/when the option is substantive & exercisable; state the correct date accordingly.

(f) 35 marks · ACC

Assuming the acquisition date is 1 June 2024, calculate the goodwill or gain on bargain purchase on the SSC group's acquisition of PED, starting from the bot's goodwill of R24 978 539.

How to answer

  1. Correct the consideration components, then apply the goodwill formula.

Model answer

  • Correct the nVestor liability to PV (FV 27.4m, n 2, i 12% → R21 843 112) and the shares to acquisition-date price (250 000 × R48.50 = R12 125 000).
  • Goodwill = consideration + NCI + previously-held 10% (at FV) − FV of identifiable net assets; recognise deferred tax on FV adjustments (except where instructed to ignore).

PAPER 1 — Mzansi (MT retailer; inventory; Inala group) · 120

(a) 16 marks · AUD

Discuss the effect of the information in section 2 (understanding the inventory balance) on the auditor's assessment of the RoMM for the inventory balance in MT's FY2025 financial statements.

Model answer

  • Demotivated staff/long hours → capture & count errors (existence, completeness, valuation); a perpetual computerised system can reduce risk — discuss both; conclude RoMM high (valuation/NRV/obsolescence).

(b) 19 marks · AUD

Describe the key controls MT should have implemented to ensure (i) the accuracy & completeness of clearance-sale data captured by Paul, and (ii) the accuracy & completeness of the promotional-discount details captured by Debra.

Model answer

  • (i) Sequentially number & review GRNs; standardised input template; logs reviewed & online-approved by the merchandise director before processing.
  • (ii) Screen edit checks: descriptive echo/drop-down, alphanumeric, sign check (no negative discount), range/reasonability on the discount %.

(c) 10 marks · AUD

Formulate the additional substantive audit procedures Sentinal should perform on the inventory obsolescence allowance at 31 December 2025.

  • Re-perform the allowance calc (accuracy); GAS compare cost vs NRV (50% of price or discounted price) to find write-downs; confirm correct mark-down %; inspect subsequent sales.

(e) 12 marks · ACC

Describe the steps AID's accounting authority should take following the media criticism of the agreement AID concluded with Inala.

Model answer

(g) 12 marks · ACC

Prepare the journal entries MT should process in its FY2025 separate financial statements to account for the Louw Investments bond (ignore tax; no narrations).

(h) 22 marks · ACC
