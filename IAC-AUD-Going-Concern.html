Going Concern — Audit Response (ISA 570)

Identify the risk → audit procedures → conclude & report · SAICA IAC 2026 · T. Van Heerden

1 · Indicators to identify the risk

  • Financial: net liability / net current-liability position · negative operating cash flows · adverse key ratios · loan defaults or covenant breaches · can't pay creditors / suppliers withdraw credit · arrear dividends · new finance unavailable.
  • Operating: loss of key management or a major customer/supplier/market/licence/franchise · labour unrest · supply shortages.
  • Other: major litigation/claims · non-compliance with laws · uninsured catastrophe · adverse changes in law/regulation.

2 · Audit procedures / response

  • Obtain & evaluate management's going-concern assessment (≥12 months from reporting date); if none done, request one.
  • Obtain the cash-flow / profit forecast and:
    • Re-perform the casts for arithmetical accuracy.
    • Agree the data/inputs to supporting evidence (audited figures, signed contracts, order book).
    • Evaluate the assumptions for reasonableness vs industry & economic conditions; corroborate with management.
    • Compare prior-year forecasts to actual results (reliability of management's forecasting) and the forecast to the latest management accounts.
  • Assess management's plans (asset sales, restructuring, cost cuts, new finance) for feasibility & likelihood; inspect supporting documents (signed agreements, board resolutions).
  • Confirm availability & terms of finance — inspect loan agreements for covenant breaches; confirm overdraft/loan facilities & continued bank support directly with the bankers.
  • Obtain a letter of support from a parent/shareholder and assess their ability to provide the support.
  • Inspect minutes of directors'/shareholders' meetings for difficulties & plans.
  • Enquire of legal counsel re litigation/claims; review events after the reporting date for further indicators/mitigations.
  • Obtain written representations (MRL) on the plans & their feasibility (ISA 570.16(e)).

3 · Conclude & report

SituationAudit report
GC appropriate · material uncertainty · adequately disclosedUnmodified opinion + "Material Uncertainty Related to Going Concern" section.
Material uncertainty not adequately disclosedQualified or adverse opinion.
GC basis inappropriate (won't continue) but AFS still on GCAdverse opinion (should use another basis).
Management refuses to assess / extend the assessmentScope limitation → consider qualified / disclaimer.
Exam tipStructure: ① identify the indicators in the scenario → ② state the procedures (forecast + facilities + support + plans + MRL) → ③ conclude on whether a material uncertainty exists → ④ state the report impact. Note management's responsibility (assess GC) vs the auditor's (evaluate it).
