Paper 1 — "Ubud" (UC: logistics-app start-up) · 120 marks

(a)(i) Date to recognise the CargoPlat™ app ACC 8

Required: from which date should the internally-generated intangible have been recognised (IAS 38)?

How: split research (expense) vs development (capitalise); capitalise only once all six development criteria are met.
Model answer:
  • Capitalise development only when the entity can demonstrate all of: technical feasibility · intention to complete · ability to use/sell · probable future economic benefits (a market exists) · adequate resources · expenditure measurable reliably (IAS 38.57).
  • Apply each criterion to UC's facts — e.g. technical feasibility was met on 2 May 2022 when the board approved the technical details of the app; identify the date the last criterion is met.
  • Recognise the intangible from that date; all spend before it (research/early development) is expensed and cannot be reinstated.
(a)(ii) Prior-period error note (IAS 8) ACC 8

Required: prepare the prior-period error note extract for FY2024.

How: prior-period error → restate comparatives retrospectively; show each affected line.
Model answer:
  • P/L & OCI: (increase) in cost of sales/amortisation (e.g. amortisation R5 010 000 ÷ 6-yr life ≈ R835 000); (decrease) in revaluation surplus (OCI).
  • SFP: adjust the intangible asset, accumulated amortisation/revaluation surplus & retained earnings for each comparative year (31/12/2023 & 31/12/2022).
  • Present each line's correction only (no totals/subtotals, no narrative) per the instructions.
(b)(i) Lease — low-value exemption (IFRS 16) ACC 4

Required: could UC validly elect the recognition exemption on the server lease?

Model answer:
  • Low-value test: (a) lessee can benefit from the asset on its own/with readily-available resources; and (b) it's not highly dependent on/interrelated with other assets (IFRS 16.B5).
  • The server's individual components only work together as one device → highly interrelated → second criterion fails.
  • Conclusion: not low-value → UC should not have elected the exemption → must recognise a right-of-use asset & lease liability.
(b)(ii) Lease — correcting journals (IFRS 16) ACC 12

Required: journals to correct the lease at 1 Jan 2024 (assume no exemption).

Model answer:
  • Lease liability = PV of payments: PMT 25 500, n = 36, i = 11.75%, 12 pmts/yr → R770 508; ROU asset = liability − lease incentive R50 000 = R720 508.
  • Dr ROU asset 720 508 / Cr Lease liability (current portion per amort) / Cr Accumulated depreciation / Cr Retained earnings (opening) — correcting prior-year recognition.
  • Raise the current-year depreciation & interest; route prior-year effects through opening retained earnings (all material).
(c) Audit vs independent review (Companies Act) AUD 11

Required: must UC be audited, or is an independent review sufficient?

Model answer:
  • s30(2)/(7) + Reg 28 & 26: audit required if public co (UC is private → no); MOI/shareholders/board require it (MOI silent; not required — but board reconsidering); holds >R5m assets in a fiduciary capacity for unrelated parties (no); JSE-subsidiary (no).
  • PIS 100–349 with internally-compiled AFS → audit. Since "Mr TAcc" resigned and the CFO took over preparation, the AFS are internally compiled → if the PIS is 100–349 an audit is required; otherwise an independent review (or exempt if all shareholders are directors).
  • Conclude based on the computed PIS & who compiled the AFS.
(d) Ethics — pre-engagement / acceptance AUD 12

Required: ethical requirements Ingxelo must consider at pre-engagement.

Model answer:
  • Acceptance considerations: client integrity, firm competence & resources, independence (identify CPC threats from the facts), contact the predecessor auditor, agree the engagement terms.
  • For each fact, name the threat (self-interest/self-review/familiarity/intimidation/advocacy) and the fundamental principle at risk, then conclude whether the engagement can be accepted.
(e) Substantive ToD — lease liability AUD 15

Required: substantive tests of detail on the lease liability (initial, subsequent & measurement, FY23 & FY24).

(f) Ratio & financial analysis (+ unit economics) MAF 30

Required: calculate profitability & liquidity ratios (incl. unit economics) and analyse.

Model answer:

(g) Criticise the free-cash-flow valuation MAF 20

Required: criticise Bongani's FCF valuation (don't re-perform).

Paper 2 — "SSC" (manufacturer; automation; acquisition of PED) · 120

(a) Capital vs revenue — automation (s11(a)) TAX 7

Required: are the robotic-automation amounts capital in nature for s11(a)?

Model answer:
  • Onus on the taxpayer (s102 TAA); "capital" undefined → use case law.
  • "Operations vs structure" test (New State Areas): cost of income-earning operations = deductible; creating an enduring benefit/structure = capital.
  • The R1.5m bot improves the income-earning structure/enduring benefit → capital, not deductible; assess the R350 000 automation similarly per its purpose; conclude per item.
(b) Corrected taxable income TAX 45

Required: recompute SSC's taxable income from PBT R29 310 000 (reasons per line).

Model answer (key adjustments):
  • Start at PBT R29 310k; each adjustment on its own line with a reason (incl. nil-effect items).
  • Amounts received in advance: only the portion unconditionally entitled is GI (earlier of receipt/accrual) — add R3.5m received; the cost to produce is deductible only in the year incurred (2025).
  • Claim the s24C allowance against income received in advance under the contract; add back the bot (capital) & claim the correct capital allowances; non-deductibles; recoupments.
(c) Report — 9 digital-strategy risks + mitigations MAF/AUD 20

Required: report describing 9 business risks of the digital strategy + a mitigation each.

Model answer:
  • Use a report format. Risks (examples): strategy not cohesive; cyber/data-security; system failure/downtime; skills/change-management; vendor lock-in; integration; cost overruns; obsolescence; reputational.
  • For each, a practical mitigation/control (governance over strategy approval, cyber controls, BCP/DR, training, dual vendors, phased rollout, monitoring).
(d) Controls over the bot's journals AUD 6

Required: controls over journal entries recorded by the bot.

Model answer:
  • Employee training; segregation of duties (bot config vs operation vs oversight); user & bot access controls (least privilege, passwords); the bot can't both create & approve.
  • Supporting documents attached & reviewed before posting; independent review/approval of bot journals; audit trail/logging; reconciliations.
(e) Acquisition date of PED (IFRS 3/10) ACC 7

Required: do you agree the acquisition date is 1 June 2024?

Model answer:
  • Acquisition date = the date the acquirer obtains control = power + exposure to variable returns + ability to use power to affect returns.
  • Per PED's MOI, power comes from voting rights; the majority-voting party has power. SSC holds 10% + an option to buy 60% → control depends on whether/when the option is substantive & exercisable.
  • State the correct acquisition date (when control actually passes) if you disagree.
(f) Goodwill / bargain purchase (IFRS 3) ACC 35

Required: calculate goodwill/bargain purchase on the PED acquisition.

Model answer:
  • Start from the bot's goodwill R24 978 539, then correct: liability to nVestor at PV (FV R27.4m, n = 2, i = 12% → R21 843 112, not R25m); shares issued at acquisition-date price (250 000 × R48.50 = R12 125 000).
  • Goodwill = consideration (corrected) + NCI + previously-held 10% interest (at FV) − FV of identifiable net assets; recognise deferred tax on FV adjustments (except where instructed to ignore).

Paper 2 — "Lexi" (individual; CreativeLexi; financing) · 120

(a) Gross income — the trip (case law) TAX 7

Required: income-tax implications of the all-expenses-paid trip.

Model answer:
  • Issue: is there an "amount, in cash or otherwise" received? "Amount" = any property with an ascertainable money value (Lategan, Brummeria).
  • The trip (MV R20 000) was a quid pro quo for services (Brummeria); par (c) GI specifically includes voluntary awards for services.
  • Conclusion: include R20 000 in Lexi's 2025 gross income; onus on Lexi (s102 TAA).
(b) Lexi's normal tax payable TAX 27

Required: calculate her 2025 normal tax.

Model answer (key lines):
  • Salary R450 000; provident-fund fringe benefit (R450 000 × 14.8%); laptop nil (mainly business, para 6(4)); cell-phone allowance included, no deduction (s23(m)/(b)); leave pay R35 000 (s7B).
  • UK secondment NOT exempt (s10(1)(o)(ii)) — <183 days & DTA upholds SA's right; interest-free study loan = nil FB; UIF not deductible.
  • Then s11F retirement deduction, taxable income → tax per table → less rebates & medical credits.
(c) Employees' tax — PSP TAX 7

Required: must Hebony withhold PAYE on the R121 500 to CreativeLexi?

(d) Interest deduction — s24J TAX 9

Required: income-tax implications of the R68,1m interest to Africa Holdings.
