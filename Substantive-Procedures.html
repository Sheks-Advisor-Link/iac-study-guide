Part A — Technical reference

A1 · What a substantive procedure is (ISA 330)

An audit procedure designed to detect material misstatement at the assertion level. It is your response to a Risk of Material Misstatement (RoMM). Two kinds:

TypeWhat it isBest for
Tests of detail (ToD)Examine the individual items making up a balance — inspect, recalculate, confirm, agree to source documents.The bulk of most answers; high-risk & estimate-heavy balances.
Substantive analytical procedures (SAP)Evaluate the balance via plausible relationships — compare to prior year/budget/ratios; build an independent expectation; investigate variances.Large, predictable populations: wages, interest, depreciation, commission.
The golden rule (every memo repeats it)Substantive procedures respond to the RoMM at the assertion level. Before writing one, ask: which account → which assertion → what could go wrong? If the question limits the assertions, stay in that lane — out-of-scope points score nothing (and can score negative where completeness is the key).

A2 · How to formulate one — mark-by-mark

A procedure that earns the mark contains four ingredients:

#IngredientSource
1Action verb — concrete & auditableThe verb bank below — never "check / ensure / look at".
2Source of evidence — the specific document/recordInvoice, GRN, contract, title deed, bank confirmation, age analysis…
3Compare-to / targetGL, TB, AFS, price list, prior-year WP, supplier statement…
4Purpose — "…to verify the [assertion] of [account]"Naming the assertion proves you understood the risk.
TEMPLATE: [Verb] the [source document] and [agree / trace / recalculate] to the [target] → to verify the [ASSERTION] of [account].

Verb bank — "I CAR ROC": InspectConfirm / CirculariseAgree / TraceRecalculate / Re-performRecomputeObserveCompareReconcileEnquire + corroborateCAATs / extract

Watch-out"Enquire" alone is the weakest evidence (oral, internal) and rarely earns the mark — always pair it: "Enquire from management… and corroborate the response by inspecting [document]."
Worked exampleRisk: revenue overstated → assertion accuracy. Procedure: "Inspect the signed sales invoice and agree the quantity to the delivery note and the price to the approved price list, recalculating qty × price less discount, to verify the accuracy of revenue."

A3 · The universal answer skeleton — apply to ANY account

ACCOUNT / BALANCE BEING TESTED ┌──────────────────┬──────────────────┬──────────────────┬───────────────────┐ ① GENERAL ("CARDS") ② TESTS OF DETAIL ③ ANALYTICAL (SAP) ④ WRAP-UP • Cast / re-perform per ASSERTION • vs prior year • Disclosure per IFRS • Agree to GL/TB/AFS at risk: • vs budget • Inspect minutes • Rep letter (MRL) E·R·A·V·C·P / • ratios & trends • Material journals • Disclosure note O·C·A·C·C·P • build expectation • Subsequent events • Start = PY balance • investigate var. • CAATs throughout
"CARDS" — the easy-mark opener (every account)
Cast / re-perform the schedule obtained from management for arithmetical accuracy  ·  Agree the total to the GL, TB & AFS  ·  Rep letter — obtain a signed written management representation on the relevant assertions (ISA 580)  ·  Disclosure — inspect the notes for compliance with the relevant IFRS/IAS  ·  Start-of-year — agree the opening balance to the prior-year audited AFS/WP.

A4 · Assertion triggers — pick the ones AT RISK

Balances (B/S) — "E-RAV-CP"Transactions (P/L) — "O-CACC-P"
Existence · Rights & obligations · Accuracy/Valuation & allocation · Completeness · PresentationOccurrence · Completeness · Accuracy · Cut-off · Classification · Presentation
Plain EnglishExistence/Occurrence = is it real & did it happen? · Completeness = anything missing? · Rights = do we own it? · Valuation = is the amount right? · Cut-off = right period? · Classification/Presentation = right place & disclosed?

Payables & accrualsCompleteness — search for unrecorded liabilities · supplier-statement recons · unmatched GRNs · cut-off · revalue FX payables.

Estimates (ISA 540)Valuation — understand process · test data · assumptions vs history/industry · build independent estimate · subsequent events · management bias.
Group / consolidationre-cast schedule · @acquisition (contract, %, goodwill/NCI) · since-acq · current-year NCI/dividends/FCTR · eliminations · disposal · component auditors.

Q1 · Trade receivables
A university recognises trade receivables (student fees) of R272m at year-end during a recessionary "fees must fall" environment; an allowance for credit losses has been raised.
Required: Describe the substantive procedures to audit the accuracy, valuation and presentation assertions of trade receivables.
Assertion / stepProcedure
General (CARDS)Obtain a schedule of receivables & credit losses; test castings/calculations; agree opening balances to PY WP and closing to AFS; inspect material adjusting journals; reference receivables in the MRL; inspect disclosure per IFRS 7 & IFRS 15.
Accuracy (CAATs)Using CAATs/GAS, extract the debtors age analysis; re-perform the ageing per invoice date (IFRS 7); agree the total to the debtors control account in the GL & TB; identify accounts exceeding their credit limit and follow up with management.
AccuracyUsing CAATs, recalculate the total value of invoices & interest charged; reconcile/match credit notes to customer accounts; re-perform a sample's opening balance + movements = closing balance.
ExistencePerform a positive external debtors confirmation (circularisation) at year-end; for non-replies, inspect the bank statement for receipts after year-end (alternative procedure).
Valuation — allowanceDiscuss the method of calculating the allowance with the CFO/TCWG; assess reasonableness given the history of bad debts & the economic climate; compare the basis to other entities in the sector / prior years; inspect minutes & policy corroborating the % applied.
Valuation — re-performObtain management's workings and re-perform all calculations; using CAATs, compute amounts outstanding beyond the policy period and compare to management's figure; stratify debtors by type and compare totals; verify integrity of the "debtor type" field by inspecting supporting documents (external funding, payment terms, default history).
Valuation — judgementEnquire whether the policy is likely to relax; back-test by comparing the prior-year allowance to bad debts actually written off after year-end.
PresentationUsing CAATs, extract debtor accounts with credit balances; investigate and reclassify to payables; inspect receivables/revenue disclosure for IFRS 7 & IFRS 15 compliance.
Analytical reviewCompare balances to prior years & budget; consider debtors-days trends vs comparable entities; investigate unexpected findings by enquiry and corroborate.
Q2 · Going concern & cash-flow forecast
Management prepared a cash-flow forecast for the next financial year to support the going-concern basis. It relies on student-number growth, planned cost cuts (no salary increases, 50% repair cut), asset sales, new commercial contracts, and additional government grants.
Required: Describe the audit procedures to assess the reasonableness of the cash-flow forecast supporting the going-concern assumption.
AreaProcedure
Obtain & understandObtain the forecast; by enquiry ascertain the process to compile it and the identity/competence of preparers (qualifications & experience).
Competence (track record)Compare forecasts these individuals prepared in prior years to the actual results that materialised.
ArithmeticRe-perform calculations (castings) for mathematical accuracy.
Data integrityAgree, by inspection, data used to supporting documents (audited prior-year figures, signed contracts).
AssumptionsEvaluate reasonableness by discussion with management and corroborate against industry norms, approved policy, economic indicators.
AnalyticalCompare forecast figures to comparable prior actuals; build an independent expectation (e.g. expected students × average fee) and assess reasonableness; multiply by historic payment % per month.
ApprovalInspect minutes of the board/audit committee where the forecast was evaluated & approved.
Specific adjustmentsConfirm the forecast includes correction of known errors (e.g. overstated grant), cash outflows for legal claims, and that non-recurring/restructuring costs are not recurring.
FundingConfirm availability of loan & overdraft facilities with bankers/credit providers.
Management's plansFor each action (no salary increases, repair cuts, asset sales, new contracts) — discuss feasibility, inspect supporting documents (union agreements, signed contracts, board resolutions, correspondence with DHET), corroborate amounts.
Wrap-upContact legal counsel (with permission) re litigation/claims; consider subsequent events; obtain a written representation on feasibility of future plans (ISA 570.16(e)).
Q3 · Impairment & value-in-use (IAS 36)
An impairment loss of ZAR74.46m was recognised on an explosives CGU. Management computed value-in-use using an 8% discount rate and a five-year projected cash-flow schedule (no terminal value); FVLCD was determined by a management expert.
Required: Describe the substantive procedures to obtain sufficient appropriate evidence about the impairment loss.
AreaProcedure
Obtain & re-performObtain management's calculation; re-perform the VIU using the 8% discount rate; agree cash flows to the approved five-year strategic sales plan.
Cash inflowsDiscuss assumptions & assess viability; agree future inflows to signed contracts for new customers and price lists/proof of future sales for existing customers; confirm non-operational/unsigned cash flows are excluded.
Cash outflowsEvaluate reasonableness vs business knowledge (fixed & variable cost of production); agree input costs to price lists/invoices at active market prices (not internal transfer prices); confirm servicing & maintenance costs are included.
Discount rateAudit reasonableness by comparing to similar listed companies / a WACC computation.
Terminal/growthDiscuss why no terminal value was included; evaluate reasonableness of the growth rate vs business knowledge & external market research.
FVLCD & expertEvaluate the management expert (ISA 500) — competence, objectivity, methods, source data, conclusions; ensure termination benefits are NOT included in costs of disposal (IAS 36); request the calculation be adjusted; re-perform.
Recoverable amountInspect that the correct recoverable amount was used — the higher of VIU and FVLCD.
BiasConsider potential manipulation of the calculation (e.g. bonus-/marketing-linked motivation).
Accounting & disclosureInspect the correcting journal posted to the GL and that the TB/AFS were adjusted; obtain MRL; inspect notes for IAS 36 disclosure; inspect minutes for approval & impairment indicators.
Q4 · Reliance on an expert (ISA 500 / ISA 620)
Management engaged an expert to determine the fair value (less costs of disposal) of a plant, arriving at R145m.
Required: Describe the procedures to evaluate reliance on the work of the management's expert.
Area (C-O-S)Procedure
ScopeInspect the engagement letter/agreement — nature, scope & objectives of the work and respective roles & responsibilities of the expert & management.
CompetenceObtain evidence of membership of an accredited professional body and qualifications; assess experience in similar valuation work (read prior reports, discuss).
ObjectivityDiscuss with management & the expert any interests/relationships threatening objectivity; obtain a declaration of independence.
Work & assumptionsUnderstand the work done & assumptions; assess appropriateness as audit evidence and alignment with the reporting framework; assess methods & conclusions vs professional judgement; assess relevance, completeness & accuracy of source data.
ConclusionsRead the report and consider whether conclusions align with underlying source documents and auditor expectations.
ErrorsWhere incorrect treatment is applied (e.g. termination benefits in costs of disposal), discuss, request adjustment and re-perform.
DisagreementIf a significant disagreement remains, appoint an independent/auditor's expert (ISA 620).
Which standard?ISA 500(8) when management used the expert (you audit their work). ISA 620 when the auditor engages their own expert.
Q5 · Capital expenditure & foreign-currency purchase (IAS 16 / IAS 21)
A new gas-extraction chamber was imported from a foreign supplier (invoice €2.5m less 10% trade discount), shipped FOB on 1 October but management used the 30 October spot rate; related freight, installation and import costs were incurred.
Required: Describe the substantive procedures that the invoice and related costs have been appropriately recognised and measured.
AssertionProcedure
GeneralObtain MRL on correct treatment of capex vs repairs; request a schedule of capitalised amounts & re-perform the casting; agree the list to the asset account; inspect evidence of CAPEX approval.
OccurrenceInspect the supplier (German GAS) invoice — made out to the entity; agree invoice detail (price, costs) to the signed purchase agreement.
DeliveryAgree invoice detail to delivery documentation; inspect proof of receipt (signature of receiving personnel); physically inspect the chamber & agree the asset number to the FAR.
ClassificationInspect all capitalised costs and confirm they qualify (directly attributable per IAS 16) and are not maintenance; identify other qualifying costs — shipping/freight, installation, testing, transit insurance, import duties.
AccuracyInspect the invoice/import contract; verify the €2.5m purchase price; verify the 10% trade discount; recalculate the net price (€2.25m).
Cut-off / recognitionInspect the bill of lading & contract to confirm the FOB date is 1 October and the asset was recognised on that date; inspect the correcting journal where the wrong spot rate (30 Oct) was used.
Accuracy — forexObtain the spot rate at 1 October from a credible source; recalculate the conversion from € to ZAR.
CompletenessInspect the FAR and confirm the asset was recognised at the correct total; follow through to the GL & AFS.
Q6 · Deferred tax asset (IAS 12)
A deferred tax asset was recognised on a subsidiary's assessed tax loss, to the extent that future taxable profit is probable. The amount is material and depends on management's budgets.
Required: List the substantive procedures to address accuracy, valuation & recoverability of the DTA.
AreaProcedure
Accuracy / valuationRequest a schedule of the DTA computation; re-perform and agree the total to the GL, TB & AFS; inspect that the DTA was computed at the correct tax rate (28%) on the estimated tax loss.
Tax loss accuracyAgree the firm's tax specialists' work on the current-year tax computation; agree amounts to those used in the DTA computation; inspect findings for errors; agree the loss brought forward to the latest SARS assessment.
Recoverability (key)Inspect the budget for future years to determine estimated taxable income against which the loss can be utilised; by enquiry & inspection, understand the budgeting process to ensure it is robust & realistic.
Budget — analytical & assumptionsPerform analytical procedures (year-on-year budget comparison; corroborate unusual changes); evaluate assumptions by enquiry & inspection; re-perform budget calculations; compare prior budgets to actual historical performance (back-test).
Approval & disclosureInspect minutes where the budget was approved; inspect notes for disclosure of recoverability uncertainty; request the MRL reference the ability to generate future taxable income.
Q7 · Non-current assets held for sale (IFRS 5)
Directors decided to sell certain assets (an investment property — FV ZAR6.75m; a head-office building — FV ZAR5.5m), accounted for under IFRS 5; fair values were determined by an external valuer (Prop Guru).
Required: Describe the substantive procedures to address the fair value of the properties included in assets held for sale.
AreaProcedure
Re-performRequest the valuation reports and re-perform all calculations and supporting workings.
Process & frameworkUnderstand the process the valuer followed to confirm compliance with IAS 40/IAS 16/IFRS 13; inspect the engagement letter (nature, scope, objectives, responsibilities).
Agree to recordsBy inspection, agree the figures in the valuation reports to the GL, TB & AFS.
RightsCompare property details (erf numbers, locations) in the reports to the title deeds to ensure the correct properties were valued.
Auditor's expertConsult the firm's own property experts to re-perform some procedures — compare FV to recent market disposals; assess reasonableness of DCFs & discount rates; agree DCF inputs to budgets, contracts & market rates.
Reliance on mgmt expertAssess the valuer's independence/objectivity and level of experience & skill (enquiry of staff, the valuer & the CFO).
Wrap-upRequest the MRL reference appropriateness of the fair values; confirm IFRS 5 held-for-sale criteria (available for immediate sale, highly probable) are met.
Q8 · PPE / rental fleet — valuation & impairment
A rental-car fleet is carried at cost less depreciation; cars are depreciated over 2 years to a 25% residual, then transferred to a used-car division. Some cars are idle (a lost contract) — a possible impairment indicator.
Required: Describe the substantive procedures on the valuation of the rental-car fleet (opening balance, impairment, residual values, depreciation, additions).
AreaProcedure
General / openingInclude a reference in the MRL on appropriateness of the fleet valuation; agree closing balances per the FAR to the GL & TB; perform analytical review of depreciation, impairments & carrying amounts vs prior year & budget; confirm the impairment/depreciation policy is the same as prior year (PY WP).
ImpairmentWhen inspecting a sample of vehicles for existence, inspect their condition for impairment indicators (damage); select monthly outlet reports & confirm all items are in the impairment calculations; use CAATs to extract an exception report of cars with extensive kilometres (impairment indicator); re-perform a sample of monthly impairment calculations; evaluate compliance with IAS 36; inspect approval signature; follow impairments to the FAR & inspect journal/GL posting.
Residual valuesAssess acceptability of the 25% residual vs industry norms; for cars transferred to the used-car division, compare the actual selling price to the 25% residual; where not sold, compare to AA guidelines/industry; discuss significant differences with management.
Depreciation & useful lifeAssess acceptability of the 2-year life vs industry norms; use CAATs to re-perform depreciation & carrying amount per the FAR; use CAATs to extract cars older than two years (exception report) and discuss with management.
Additions (new cars)Use CAATs to identify cars purchased in the year; for a sample, agree cost to the invoice & supporting documents; confirm VAT and registration costs are correctly capitalised; confirm repairs, maintenance & annual licence fees are NOT capitalised.
Exception reportsUse CAATs to extract negative cost prices/depreciation/carrying amounts and cases where opening + movement ≠ closing; audit & resolve with management.
Q9 · Payroll & wages (substantive analytical)
Weekly wages are a large, predictable expense; the number of employees rose from 80 to 300 during the year.
Required: Describe the substantive analytical procedures to test the payroll expense for weekly wages.
AreaProcedure
ComparisonsCompare wage expense month-to-month/week-to-week for the current year; to prior-year figures; to budgets & forecasts; and per cost centre. Compare deductions paid to third parties month-to-month; compare average wage per employee per month to prior year/budget.
ExpectationDevelop an independent expectation to compare payroll against — e.g. the increase in employees from 80 to 300 × average wage.
Follow-upFollow up on fluctuations arising from the comparisons with management and corroborate.
Ratio & trendWages as a % of total expenses / total sales / production costs; wages in relation to output/production.
Q10 · Net investment in leases — lessor (IFRS 16)
A lessor recognises a "net investment in leases" for finance-leased vehicles, generated from a leasing system (FMSS). You may rely on the completeness/accuracy of the lease data loaded.
Required: Describe the substantive procedures to verify the valuation of the net investment in leases.
AreaProcedure
GeneralObtain a signed MRL covering completeness, accuracy, valuation & allocation; obtain a system report per contract (asset value, gross investment, unearned finance income, opening/closing net investment, interest, payments); re-perform the casting; agree the closing total to the GL & AFS; agree the opening balance to the PY audited AFS.
Valuation (CAATs)Using CAATs, recalculate the unearned finance income per contract & extract differences; recalculate the gross investment (undiscounted future lease payments receivable); recalculate net investment = gross − unearned, and extract differences.
TermsBy enquiry & inspection of contracts, verify addendums/changes (extension/early-termination options) and consider the reasonableness of estimates re the likelihood of options being exercised.
Sample of contractsInspect the contract & verify fixed (in-substance fixed) payments and any variable payments linked to a rate/index; inspect the report to verify no other variable payments were included; agree payments received to the bank statement & ledger allocation.
Residual valuesInspect contracts for residual-value guarantees; use an expert for expected residual values of similar assets; inspect industry publications/trade values.
Impairment (IFRS 9)Consider whether impairment is required; review the overdue-payment report for the extent of impairment; discuss triggers increasing credit risk; corroborate via default evidence; enquire which accounts were handed over for collection & inspect they are impaired.
ConfirmationWith permission, contact the lessees and request confirmation of amounts owing.
Q11 · Cash bonus / incentive scheme
A company declared a cash bonus to eligible employees, triggered by achieving R550m target sales (10%) and health-&-safety standards. Procedures were performed after year-end; you may rely on payroll figures.
Required: Describe the substantive procedures on the cash incentive scheme.
AreaProcedure
GeneralObtain the scheme calculation/schedule & re-perform; obtain a signed MRL on all relevant assertions; agree the amount to the GL/TB/AFS; inspect notes for disclosure (IFRS 2/IAS 19/IAS 24/IAS 37).
Existence / termsInspect board minutes (and the announcement) to confirm the bonus was declared & verify terms (target sales, %, health-&-safety standards, eligible employees) & approval.
Trigger metInspect actual audited revenue in the working papers to confirm the R550m target; inspect health-&-safety audit reports (number, content — "clean"?).
Eligibility / occurrence / completenessObtain a list of recipients; trace a sample to payroll/employment contracts to confirm eligibility & employment at the qualifying date; trace to later payrolls to confirm still employed (occurrence); trace from payroll to the list (completeness).
AccuracyFor a sample, agree salary used to payroll; agree total salary to the SoP/L; recalculate each employee's % (ratio); recalculate the cash portion (individual ÷ total salary × pool); agree the amount paid to bank statements; follow up & corroborate discrepancies.
Q12 · Group statements / consolidations
A parent acquired a controlling interest in a subsidiary (some foreign); the consolidated AFS include goodwill, NCI, FCTR and intercompany balances. A subsidiary was also disposed of in the separate AFS.
Required: Describe the substantive procedures over the consolidation (acquisition, since-acquisition, current year, and disposal).

General / standard

  • Obtain management's consolidation working schedules & re-perform for mathematical accuracy (esp. NCI); inspect for unusual entries & corroborate; agree totals to the audited AFS; agree posting of consolidation journals to the GL; obtain an MRL on the completeness & accuracy of the consolidation; confirm functional currency (IAS 21) & cost/fair-value policy.
StageProcedure
@ AcquisitionInspect the purchase contract (date, consideration → agree to bank statement, shares); recalculate % shareholding; confirm control/significant influence (IFRS 10/11/IAS 28); inspect MOIs & minutes for the s115 special resolution; inspect acquiree's audited AFS for reserves at acquisition; confirm NCI policy (FV or proportionate); engage an expert to re-perform FVs of NCI & identifiable assets/liabilities; recalculate goodwill/gain on bargain purchase & agree to the consolidated AFS; inspect journals eliminating the investment & raising goodwill/NCI.
Since-acquisitionInspect audited AFS for opening reserves; re-perform additional/less depreciation & profit/loss on intercompany asset sales; recalculate "since" reserves; confirm NCI received its share.
Current yearAgree the subsidiary's profit to its audited AFS; recalculate the NCI portion & agree to the consolidated SoP/L & OCI; for dividends — inspect minutes (authorisation, date, solvency & liquidity test) & confirm intercompany elimination; for FCTR — obtain rates from a financial institution, recalculate FCTR on/excluding goodwill & the current-year movement, agree to the SoCiE.
IntercompanyInspect workings to confirm elimination of unrealised profits, intercompany loans & interest, intergroup debtors/creditors, and intercompany dividends.
Disposal (separate AFS)Obtain MRL & a schedule of proceeds, CA & P/L; inspect the sales agreement (parties/signatures, amount, effective date, terms, % disposed); if to a related party assess arm's length (ISA 550); agree CA to PY WP & re-perform; recalculate P/L (or reclassify the mark-to-market reserve if at FV); recalculate any loan PV & interest (IFRS 9); inspect derecognition in the GL; inspect IAS 24 disclosure in both AFS.
Q13 · Reliance on component auditors (ISA 600R)
A significant component of the group is audited by a different (component) auditor; the group auditor wishes to rely on their work.
Required: Formulate the substantive procedures to ensure the group auditor obtained the necessary information to rely on the component auditor's work.
RefProcedure
.23 / A57–A58Enquire whether the group auditor can be sufficiently & appropriately involved in the component auditor's work; specifically enquire whether the component auditor is subject to restrictions limiting communication / sharing of documentation, and whether they will cooperate & perform the work requested.
.25Enquire whether the component auditor understands & will comply with relevant ethical requirements, including independence.
.26Inspect documents / enquire of third parties indicating the component auditor's professional competence & capabilities.
.29Enquire whether the component auditor commits to meeting the group auditor's expectations, including communication at appropriate times throughout the audit.
.28Inspect & evaluate the component auditor's working papers (adequacy, sufficiency & appropriateness of evidence obtained).
